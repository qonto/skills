🎯 Why this matters

On September 1st, 2026, every French VAT-registered company must be able to receive electronic invoices (issuing: 2026 for large companies & mid-caps, 2027 for SMEs). The trap isn't the date — it's the client file: no SIREN, no intra-EU VAT number, no structured address = no valid Factur-X. The skill turns the Qonto account into a readiness check-up:

1

Readiness score

Client file complete for Factur-X, invoice quality, timeline known, e-reporting mapped — transparent formula, before/after.

2

Your timeline

Company size fetched from the public registry (large/mid-cap/SME) or asked, never guessed → your issuing date, 2026 or 2027.

3

E-reporting exposure

Foreign and B2C clients spotted in the records AND the real money flows: outside e-invoicing, but data to transmit.

4

Live fix

SIRENs recovered via the public registry API (no key), line-by-line preview → update_client after your confirmation.

Would someone run this on a Monday morning? With a legal deadline counting down for every French business, it's the Monday-morning question of 2026.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • Trade name ≠ legal name: registry search by name + zip code; no match → the skill asks for the SIREN or skips the record — never an auto-match
  • Registry homonyms: candidates shown (name, city, SIREN), the user picks — systematically
  • Intra-EU VAT derived from the SIREN (FR key + 2 digits): always labeled "derived — to be confirmed", never presented as verified
  • Address dumped as one text blob → counted as incomplete, flagged for restructuring
  • No client records, registry down, non-FR country: the skill says what it can and can't do, inventing nothing
  • Pagination ≤ 50 everywhere · get_organization always first (list_transactions requires bank_account_id/iban)

🛡 Guardrails

  • An audit, not legal or tax advice — stated in every report; edge cases → the company's accountant
  • The PDP choice belongs to the user: criteria provided, decision never made for them
  • Registry homonyms → confirmation, always; derived VAT numbers labeled as derived
  • update_client: full preview + explicit confirmation, field by field, never a silent bulk update; failures reported, never presented as done
  • Report examples invented and stated as such · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere · graceful degradation (empty account, non-FR country, registry down)
