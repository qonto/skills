# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-vat-return

Invoke with `/qonto-vat-return <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started
- "Prepare my VAT return for June"
- "How much VAT do I owe this month?"

## Going further
- "Fill in my CA3 box by box, with the justification for each amount"
- "Break down my collected VAT per rate for last month"
- "Which debits have no VAT information this period? How solid is my deductible total?"
- "Compare this quarter's VAT to the previous quarters — anything off?"
- "Any EU purchases or foreign suppliers I should flag for reverse charge?"
- "Am I on monthly or quarterly filing? Detect it from my payment history"

## Chain it
- "Provision that amount so it's ready on the deadline" — hand over to `qonto-tax-pilot`
- "Find the receipts behind the untagged debits before I file" — run `qonto-receipt-hunter`
- "Now close the whole month" — continue with `qonto-monthly-close`
