qonto-meeting-invoice

From call to quote, before the meeting ends — careful extraction, draft first, send after confirmation.

📝 Nothing invented, everything quoted🔒 Draft first, confirmed send 🧾 Legal mentions = Qonto settings📄 PR-ready

🎯 Why this matters

A great sales call, everyone's excited… and the quote goes out three days later. By then the momentum is gone. Call-to-invoice is a known dream — this skill's bet is making it trustworthy: what wasn't said on the call never enters the document.

1

Transcript in

A file, a Notion/Drive note (when that MCP is connected), or pasted text — no third-party MCP required.

2

Careful extraction

Deliverables, quantities, prices, discounts, terms — only what was said, every term quoted. The rest: a question, never a guess.

3

Matched against Qonto

Product catalog (prices, VAT), client records (confirmed creation for new prospects), duplicate check on existing quotes.

4

Draft, then send

Quote (or invoice) as a draft, line-by-line recap, sent only after an explicit "yes, send it".

Would someone use this on a Monday morning? Every call that ends with "I'll send you a quote" is this skill's Monday morning.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • Quoted extraction, never guessed: every extracted term is backed by its transcript line — filler words, several speakers and mixed languages are the normal case
  • "The usual rate" → matched against the catalog (list_products) and confirmed; call/catalog divergences flagged ("the call says €850/day, your catalog says €900"), never settled silently
  • No catalog? → lines built from the call, missing prices asked one by one
  • New prospect with half an address → create_client proposed with known fields read back, missing ones asked; list_clients first (duplicate check)
  • Payment term not stated → asked, or the organization's Qonto default offered — explicitly, never silently
  • Non-French organization → same workflow; VAT and legal mentions follow that country's Qonto settings, nothing French is assumed · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere

🛡 Guardrails

  • Documents created via MCP are real: draft first, always; never sent without explicit confirmation; never presented as sent while it is a draft
  • Nothing invented: a price, VAT rate, quantity or due date missing from the call → a question — no default VAT
  • create_client only after details are read back; list_clients first (duplicate check)
  • Rehearsals: fictitious client + delete_quote / delete_client_invoice (drafts only — a finalized invoice cannot be deleted)
  • Pagination ≤ 50 · personal data masked · no legal or tax advice — Qonto settings and the accountant remain the reference
