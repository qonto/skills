# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-money-calendar

Invoke with `/qonto-money-calendar <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started

- "Put my money month in my calendar — everything to pay and everything to collect."
- "What's due this week?"

## Going further

- "Add my next-quarter deadlines to the calendar, amounts in the event titles."
- "Which recurring charges did you detect from my history — rent, subscriptions, tax debits?"
- "Show me the recap before creating anything — I want to drop a few lines."
- "How many days tagged [Acme] did I log this quarter? Just count, don't invoice."
- "Re-run the schedule — update the existing events without duplicating them."
- "Invoice my June: count the tagged days and draft the invoice at my usual day rate."

## Chain it

- "Draft the invoice from my tagged days, then keep an eye on it with qonto-invoice-chaser if it goes unpaid."
- "For the client meetings that should become invoices, hand over to qonto-meeting-invoice."
- "Cross-check these deadlines against my tax calendar with qonto-tax-radar."

> ⚠️ Calendar events are only created after you confirm the recap, and invoices are drafted — never sent — with a day rate you explicitly confirm.
