# French micro-entrepreneur rates and thresholds

Last reviewed: November 2026, against service-public.fr pages F36232 (rates) and F32353 (ceilings), verified there on 1 January 2026. These figures change by decree, usually on 1 January and sometimes on 1 July.
Always tell the user to compare with the rate shown in their URSSAF account (autoentrepreneur.urssaf.fr) and the
thresholds published on urssaf.fr and impots.gouv.fr. The figure the user reads there wins.

## Social contributions (share of revenue received)

| Activity | Rate | Training contribution (CFP) | Versement libératoire (if opted in) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales of goods, food on premises, hotel accommodation (BIC) | 12.3 % | 0.1 % traders, 0.3 % craftsmen | 1 % |
| Commercial or craft services (BIC) | 21.2 % | 0.1 % traders, 0.3 % craftsmen | 1.7 % |
| Non-regulated liberal activity under SSI (BNC) | 25.6 % in 2026 (24.6 % in 2025) | 0.2 % | 2.2 % |
| Regulated liberal professions under CIPAV (BNC) | 23.2 % | 0.2 % | 2.2 % |

- The training contribution (CFP) is declared on top of the rate on autoentrepreneur.urssaf.fr; some summaries
  present a single all-in rate. Use what the user's account shows.
- Traders and craftsmen registered with a chamber may also owe the chamber tax (taxe pour frais de chambre, CCI or
  CMA), collected with the contributions, with exemptions depending on revenue: read it in the URSSAF account. The
  skill never adds it to its subtotal.
- ACRE lowers the rates during the first months of activity. Its scope and percentage changed in 2025 and 2026:
  use the reduced rate shown in the URSSAF account, never an assumed one.
- Furnished rentals (classified tourist rentals or not) and bed and breakfasts have their own contribution rates,
  versement libératoire rates and ceilings, changed by the 2024 law on tourist rentals. They are out of scope: ask for
  the rate shown in the URSSAF account and the ceiling given by the accountant.

## Micro-enterprise ceilings (annual revenue)

| Activity | Ceiling |
|---|---|
| Sales of goods, food and hotel accommodation | 203 100 EUR |
| Services (BIC and BNC) | 83 600 EUR |

- Figures for 2026 (they were 188 700 EUR and 77 700 EUR from 2023 to 2025), revised every three years.
- The ceilings are assessed on revenue excluding VAT.
- Mixed activity: total revenue under 203 100 EUR and the services part under 83 600 EUR.
- First year: prorate the ceilings by days of activity.
- The micro regime is lost after two consecutive calendar years above the ceiling.

## VAT franchise thresholds (franchise en base de TVA)

| Activity | Base threshold | Majorated threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Sales of goods, food and hotel accommodation | 85 000 EUR | 93 500 EUR |
| Services | 37 500 EUR | 41 250 EUR |

- Figures in force in 2026 (service-public.fr F21746); the single 25 000 EUR threshold planned in 2025 was abandoned.
- First year: these thresholds apply in full, without prorating, to decide whether VAT is due during that year.
  The first year's revenue is annualised only to decide the regime of the following year.
- Mixed activity: the total must stay under the goods thresholds and the services part under the services ones.
- The VAT base follows the operations, not the cash: goods delivered but unpaid count, and some services to
  businesses abroad are not taxable in France and may not count. Bank credits only give an indication.
- Lawyers, authors and performing artists have their own thresholds: ask.
- Crossing the majorated threshold: VAT is due on the operations from the day it is crossed.
- Crossing only the base threshold: the franchise ends on 1 January of the following year.

## Declaration deadlines

- Monthly: the last day of the month after the period.
- Quarterly: 30 April, 31 July, 31 October and 31 January.
- First declaration: its period and deadline differ; use those shown in the URSSAF account.
- A declaration is due even when revenue is zero.
