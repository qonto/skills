# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-prescription-guard

Invoke with `/qonto-prescription-guard <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started

- "Which of my unpaid invoices are at risk of becoming time-barred?"
- "Is this old invoice from 2023 still legally recoverable?"

## Going further

- "How many euros become unrecoverable within the next 12 months?"
- "This client is a consumer, not a business — recompute the deadline for their invoice."
- "That client sent me a small transfer in March — does it reset the clock on their debt?"
- "I have a written acknowledgment of debt from this client — factor it into the schedule."
- "Show me my receivables as a timeline, most urgent first."
- "List the invoices that are probably already time-barred, separately from the living ones."

## Chain it

- "Chase the red-flagged invoices before they die — hand over to qonto-invoice-chaser."
- "Reminders already failed on the most urgent one — build the court file with qonto-injonction."
- "Re-run the audit next quarter and tell me what changed."
