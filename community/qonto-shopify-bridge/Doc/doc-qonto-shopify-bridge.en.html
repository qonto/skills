qonto-shopify-bridge

What the store claims vs what the bank collects — e-commerce payout reconciliation for your Qonto account.

🌉 Multi-MCP: Qonto + Shopify🔒 Zero writes — pure read 📦 Grouped payouts & 2–3-day delays handled📄 PR-ready

🎯 Why this matters

Every Shopify merchant lives with the same blind spot: gross sales in the store dashboard, net payouts on the bank account — and between the two, fees, refunds and delays. Most merchants never reconcile; they just trust. This skill checks:

1

Payout rhythm

Each PSP's cadence (Shopify, Stripe, PayPal, SumUp…) learned from history — every break becomes an alert: missing or late payout.

2

Real fees vs announced

Effective rate computed payout by payout: (gross − net) ÷ gross, compared to your plan's advertised rate when you provide it. No invented schedules.

3

Refunds & chargebacks

Refunded in the store but never reflected on the account (or the reverse): detected, documented, referenced.

4

The true margin

Net cash collected per period after every commission — not the flattering number on the store dashboard.

The typical report: "14 payouts expected, 13 received, 1 late by 4 days; effective fees 2.9% + €0.30, in line with the plan; net collected this month: €X". Would someone use this on a Monday morning? That's exactly when last week's payouts should have landed.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • Grouped payouts handled: one transfer = n orders − fees − refunds; the skill reconstructs the group by date window + amount proximity, never naive same-day equality
  • 2–3 business-day banking delays: the Qonto date is settled_at — the skill matches by window, not by the date the store claims
  • PayPal manual withdrawals: the on-demand pattern is recognized and requalified — no false "missing payout" alarms
  • Multi-store & multi-PSP: one reconciliation lane per store and per provider, consolidated summary on top
  • Multi-currency: reported per currency, never converted silently
  • No Shopify MCP → Qonto-only mode announced; history < 3 months → low confidence announced; pagination ≤ 50 everywhere

🛡 Guardrails

  • Pure read: no write tool, nothing created or modified — on the Qonto side or the store side
  • Never invents a fee schedule: computed rates only, sample size shown
  • A "missing payout" is a documented hypothesis, not an accusation — support has the final word
  • Per-currency reporting, no silent conversion · IBANs masked (last 4 digits) · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere
  • Honest degradation: no Shopify MCP → Qonto-only announced; thin history → low confidence announced
