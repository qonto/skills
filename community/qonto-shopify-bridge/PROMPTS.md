# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-shopify-bridge

Invoke with `/qonto-shopify-bridge <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started
- "Did all my Shopify payouts actually arrive last week?"
- "Reconcile my store sales with my bank account"

## Going further
- "What do Stripe fees really cost me, payout by payout?"
- "Is a payout missing or late this week? Compare against the usual rhythm"
- "What's my net cash collected this month, after all commissions?"
- "Were March's refunds actually passed through to my account?"
- "Reconcile each of my stores separately — one lane per store"
- "Has my effective fee rate drifted over the last six months?"

## Chain it
- "Draft the support ticket for the missing payout, with all the references"
- "Put the true net margin into my company dashboard" — open `qonto-ceo-cockpit`
- "Include these numbers in this month's investor update" — continue with `qonto-board-pack`
