The complete recurring-spend audit — what you pay, what you forgot, what quietly went up, and what just started.

🔍 Pure Qonto🔒 Zero Qonto writes 🌱 Early detection of newborns📊 Dashboard in Claude

🎯 Why this matters

Subscriptions pile up, prices creep up silently, trials convert, nobody cancels. The skill runs the COMPLETE recurring-spend audit over 24–36 months:

1

The full inventory

Every recurring charge (cards AND direct debits), monthly/quarterly/yearly cadence, the TOTAL annualized cost — the shock number — and a status per line: ✅ active · 👥 duplicate · 🧟 zombie (user-confirmed) · 🎣 trial gone paid.

2

The newborns 🌱

Notorious subscription merchants — SaaS, AI, course platforms (Systeme.io, Skool…), hosting & domain registrars (OVH, Gandi…) — recognized from the very first charge, no history needed; then round amounts, label hints, 2nd occurrence ~30 d → "probable subscription, to confirm". Catch the trial before it becomes rent.

3

Every hike detected

Price plateaus (±2 %, a jump persisting ≥ 2 cycles), FX and VAT excluded, 🟢🟡🔴 tags vs inflation, ⚪ variable usage kept apart — plus the cumulated annual overcost of all hikes.

4

Dashboard + emails

A dashboard built directly in Claude, exportable as a self-contained HTML file in Qonto colors; an argued negotiation email and a monthly digest — Gmail drafts, never sent without you.

Would someone use this on a Monday morning? It's the audit every founder postpones because it means opening 30 months of statements. Here it's one prompt.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • Counterparty normalization: references, dates and card-processor prefixes (PAYPAL *, SUMUP *…) stripped; SEPA and card variants of the same supplier merged into one line
  • Yearly lines no longer fly under the radar: 24–36 month target window, the 350–380 d cadence is recognized — and below 24 months the skill says yearly lines may be missing instead of pretending
  • Steps, not noise: a plateau = ≥ 2 stable charges (±2 %); a hike = a jump that persists ≥ 2 cycles. Single outliers, prorated first months, FX wobble and VAT-rate changes excluded by design
  • VAT neutralized: excl-VAT comparison when list_supplier_invoices has the matching invoice — a tax change is never blamed on the supplier
  • Variable usage said plainly: "implicit price not isolable from payment data" rather than a fake conclusion; 🌱 and 🧟 are hypotheses to confirm, never verdicts
  • Short history, empty account, no recurring charges: the skill states what it can see and what it can't yet · cadence math on emitted_at · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere

🛡 Guardrails

  • Zero Qonto writes — reads only; the capped card is qonto-subscription-guardian territory
  • 🧟 is a hypothesis, always user-confirmed; 🌱 is "probable, to confirm", never asserted
  • Never accuses a supplier when the data says usage, FX or VAT — doubtful lines go to variable usage with the reason
  • Emails are drafts: never sent, no recipient added without the user; payment-data estimates ≠ contract audit
  • Honest degradation under 24 months of history (yearly lines may be missing — and the skill says so) · masked IBANs · pagination ≤ 50
