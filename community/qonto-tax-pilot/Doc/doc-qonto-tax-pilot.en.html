🎯 Why this matters

The number-one cash killer for small businesses isn't revenue — it's VAT money that was already spent when the notice arrives. The skill turns a Qonto account into a tax copilot:

1

Dated tax schedule

When each tax is due and how much — VAT, corporate tax instalments & balance, CFE, dividend flat tax — confidence-tagged 🟢🟡🔵 per line.

2

90-day projection

Day-by-day cash per account: dated low point, threshold crossings, what-if simulations.

3

Monthly provision

Net VAT (on receipts when detected!) + IS/12 + CFE/12 — full breakdown shown.

4

One safe action

A transfer request to the tax sub-account — money moves only after SCA approval in the app.

Would someone use this on a Monday morning? It's literally a first-of-the-month ritual.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • VAT regime AND filing frequency detected, never asked: monthly DGFIP debits → monthly CA3; quarterly → quarterly CA3 (annual VAT < €4,000); July + December instalments → simplified (CA12, abolished 2027-01-01 — 2025 Finance Act, switch announced); vat_payment_condition: "on_receipts" → VAT follows client payments, not issued invoices
  • Multi-year analysis: up to 36 months, covering at least the two previous fiscal years — tax cadences only show across full years
  • Country detection: full tax calendar for France; other Qonto countries → generic projection + provisioning, stated plainly
  • Deductible VAT: summed vat_amount — announced as a floor, with the count of untagged debits disclosed
  • Empty account, no sub-account, non-French company: the skill says what it can and can't estimate instead of pretending
  • Pagination ≤ 50 everywhere, card settlement delays (emitted_at vs settled_at), refunds deducted from collected VAT

🛡 Guardrails

  • Never creates a transfer request without explicit confirmation in the current conversation; never presented as executed
  • Estimates ≠ filings — accountant validation recommended in every report
  • Honest degradation under 6 months of history · masked IBANs · pagination ≤ 50
  • Rehearsals: decline_request after each test (request_type: "multi_transfers", plural)
