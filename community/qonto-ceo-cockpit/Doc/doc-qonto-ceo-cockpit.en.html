qonto-ceo-cockpit

"My Company" — the founder's cockpit, generated on demand from the Qonto account.

📖 100 % read-only🎛 Live recalc, no MCP call 👁 Presentation mode📄 PR-ready

🎯 Why this matters

A bank shows you a list of lines. To actually see your company — where the money comes from, where it goes, where you'll stand in three months — you need a spreadsheet… or three weeks of your accountant's time. The skill does it in one prompt: "show me my company" → an interactive, self-contained HTML dashboard:

1

Flow view

Revenue sources → company → spending categories (Sankey-style), ribbons ∝ amounts, month/year toggle, tooltips.

2

Cards & 3-month forecast

Month / year / forecast per category + KPIs: consolidated balance, month net, tax vault, 3 months — 🟢 real / 🟡 estimated.

3

Live day-rate hypotheses

Day rate detected from invoices × billable days: sliders, instant in-page recalc — zero new MCP calls.

4

Eye toggle 👁

One click hides a sensitive line and recomputes totals — present without showing everything (banker meeting).

Would someone use this on a Monday morning? It's the Monday-morning screen: open, look, decide.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • No labels? Grouping falls back to normalized counterparties, clearly announced — plus an explicit "Uncategorized" bucket with its transaction count, never silently spread
  • Internal transfers netted out: moves between the org's own accounts are neither income nor spend; the one to the tax sub-account feeds the vault gauge
  • No day-based invoicing? The hypothesis panel is omitted, with the reason — the rest of the cockpit is complete
  • No tax sub-account? Vault gauge hidden, everything else intact
  • Empty or young account? Honest empty-state cockpit: 🟡 tags, a warning, never an invented number
  • Pagination ≤ 50 everywhere, 3-month windows · non-French company → neutral tax naming, zero invented local rules

🏗 Functional diagram

🔒

100 % read-only: no write, no transfer, nothing to approve. The deliverable is a local, self-contained HTML file — the data never leaves the machine, no external code loads. The eye toggle 👁 is a presentation convenience (the data stays in the file's source); for sharing, the skill regenerates an export where hidden lines are genuinely excluded.

🛡 Guardrails

  • 100 % read-only skill: no write tool, no transfer, nothing to approve — said plainly when asked
  • Eye toggle 👁 = presentation, not security: never presented as a redacted document; a genuinely excluded export is offered for sharing
  • Forecast and hypotheses are 🟡 estimates, not accounting — accountant validation recommended
  • IBANs masked (last 4 digits) · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere · never an invented category, tax rule, or number
