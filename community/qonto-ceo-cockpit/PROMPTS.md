# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-ceo-cockpit

Invoke with `/qonto-ceo-cockpit <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started
- "Show me my company"
- "Build my CEO dashboard"

## Going further
- "Where does my money come from and where does it go this year?"
- "What if I raise my day rate to €700 — what does the year look like?"
- "And if I only bill 12 days a month instead of 15?"
- "What's my 3-month forecast, with the estimated parts clearly tagged?"
- "Prepare the banker version — hide the sensitive lines, then regenerate with them truly excluded"
- "Refresh the cockpit with this morning's data"

## Chain it
- "Now dig into the subscription line" — audit it with `qonto-subscription-audit`
- "Zoom into the tax deadlines on the timeline" — get the full schedule from `qonto-tax-pilot`
- "Turn this into a proper monthly update for my investors" — continue with `qonto-board-pack`
