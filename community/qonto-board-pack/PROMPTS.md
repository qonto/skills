# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-board-pack

Invoke with `/qonto-board-pack <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started
- "Prepare my monthly investor update"
- "Build June's pack for my banker"

## Going further
- "Banker version, trends only — no exact figures"
- "What were last month's highlights? Just the detection, no formatting yet"
- "How does my burn and runway compare to the trailing twelve months?"
- "Compare my spend by category over the last three months"
- "Rewrite the outlook paragraph with a more cautious tone before you format anything"
- "Same numbers, internal version — everything, unfiltered"

## Chain it
- "Draft the email to the investors with the pack attached — I'll review and send"
- "Pull the true e-commerce margin into the pack first" — reconcile with `qonto-shopify-bridge`
- "Give me the interactive version for my own screen" — open `qonto-ceo-cockpit`

> ⚠️ The narrative stops for your review before any formatting, and the recap email is only ever a draft — nothing leaves without you.
