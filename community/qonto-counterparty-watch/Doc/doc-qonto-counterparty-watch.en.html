qonto-counterparty-watch

Know who owes you money… and who's sinking — a counterparty radar for your Qonto account.

🔍 100% read-only, zero writes🇫🇷 SIRENE + BODACC cross-checked 🤝 Datagouv optional, auto-detected📄 PR-ready

🎯 Why this matters

An unpaid invoice hurts. An unpaid invoice from a client already in receivership may be gone forever — and nobody told you. The skill turns a Qonto account into a counterparty radar:

1

Exposure per counterparty

Unpaid client invoices (amounts, 0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+ ageing), supplier commitments, annualized recurring spend.

2

Payment behavior

Observed payment date vs due_date: average delay, worst delay, worsening trend per client.

3

Legal health

SIRENE (deregistrations, cessations) + BODACC (receivership, liquidation, safeguard), dated — when the Datagouv MCP is present.

4

Justified 🟢🟡🔴 score

Exposure × legal health × delay → cross-signal alerts: "X owes you N € AND just entered a procedure — chase today".

Would someone run this on a Monday morning? That's exactly when you triage who to chase this week.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • Counterparty name normalization: bank labels are messy (SEPA noise, payment references, SARL / SAS / EURL suffixes) — Qonto client records and transaction counterparties are merged on a normalized key, with the raw label kept for display
  • SIREN extracted, never guessed: a French VAT number is 2 keys + a 9-digit SIREN on client records; without a SIREN → exact-name match flagged as lower confidence, or "not verifiable"
  • Honest about coverage: "✅ verified on BODACC on DD/MM, nothing found" ≠ "guaranteed healthy" — coverage through data.gouv can be partial and the skill says so
  • No Datagouv MCP: announced once, Qonto-pure scoring delivered (exposure + measured delays are already actionable)
  • Empty account, no invoices, non-French counterparties: the skill says what it can and can't assess instead of pretending
  • Pagination ≤ 50 everywhere · multi-account organizations handled per account · masked IBANs

🏗 Functional diagram

🔍

100% read-only, zero writes: the skill calls no Qonto write tool — nothing to approve, nothing to execute. It crosses two worlds that never talk to each other: your Qonto account (who owes you what) and the public registries (who is going under). Data flows out; nothing flows in.

🛡 Guardrails

  • 100% read-only — no Qonto write tool is ever called; nothing to approve, nothing to execute
  • Never a legal status without source + date; "nothing found" ≠ "guaranteed healthy" when coverage is partial
  • Alerts are signals, not legal advice: open procedure → confirm on bodacc.fr and talk to a lawyer/accountant
  • Honest degradation: no Datagouv → Qonto-pure scoring, announced; non-French counterparties → exposure + delays only
  • IBANs masked (last 4 digits) · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere
