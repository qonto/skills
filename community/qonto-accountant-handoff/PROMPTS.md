# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-accountant-handoff

Invoke with `/qonto-accountant-handoff <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started
- "Prepare the June pack for my accountant"
- "What's missing before I send the month to my accountant?"

## Going further
- "Prepare the whole Q2 pack — statements, invoices, receipts status"
- "How is receipt coverage looking this month, without generating the full pack?"
- "Annotate the transactions factually — refunds matched, internal transfers named, duplicates flagged"
- "Rework the handoff letter: add my question about the leasing contract"
- "File everything into the usual year/month folder on my Drive"
- "My accountant asked for March's supporting documents — pull that month together"

## Chain it
- "Hunt down the missing receipts before we send anything" — run `qonto-receipt-hunter` on the list
- "Run the quality check on the month first" — start with `qonto-monthly-close`
- "Draft the recap email to the firm with the folder link — I'll hit send"

> ⚠️ Zero writes on your account: the pack is filed and the recap email is drafted only — you review everything and send it yourself.
