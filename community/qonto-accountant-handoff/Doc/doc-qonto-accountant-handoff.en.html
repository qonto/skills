qonto-accountant-handoff

The monthly handoff to your accountant, minus the chore — the complete pack, prepared from your Qonto account.

📚 Zero writes on Qonto🗂 YYYY/MM Drive filing ✉️ Gmail draft — never auto-sent📄 PR-ready

🎯 Why this matters

Every month, the same message: "could you send me March's documents?" — followed by an evening of digging through emails and downloads. The skill builds the complete handoff pack straight from the Qonto account:

1

Statements & invoices, gathered

The period's official statements + every client and supplier invoice, with statuses.

2

Receipt completeness report

Each transaction checked against its attachments: ✅ attached · ⚠️ missing · ➖ not needed — with the precise missing list.

3

Annotated, never booked

Labels, VAT, factual notes ("this refund corresponds to…") — no accounting entry is ever invented.

4

Letter + organized filing

The month in ten lines, the attention points, the questions; a YYYY/MM Drive tree + a Gmail draft.

Would someone use this on a Monday morning? It's literally the first Monday of the month.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • Month not closed yet: a partial pack, clearly labelled, statements listed as "pending month close" — never presented as complete
  • Missing receipts: the headline of the letter, not a footnote — precise list (date · counterparty · amount) + a pointer to qonto-receipt-hunter
  • Untagged VAT: the VAT view is announced as a floor, with the count of untagged debits disclosed
  • Refunds and internal transfers: matched and named factually ("reverses the May 12 payment"), never allocated to an account
  • No Drive/Gmail: the same pack lands as a structured local folder — the skill says so and carries on
  • Empty account or an account opened mid-month: the skill says what exists and what doesn't · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere · card settlement delays (emitted_at vs settled_at) handled at month boundaries

🏗 Functional diagram

🔒

Zero writes on Qonto: on the Qonto side, everything is a read — nothing to approve. The only two "writes" happen on the user's side: a folder filed on their Google Drive (announced first), and a draft in their Gmail — the user always hits send themselves. And the skill does not do the accounting: factual annotation only, the accountant stays the professional.

🛡 Guardrails

  • The skill does not do the accounting: no journal entries, no chart-of-accounts allocation, no tax advice — factual annotation only, the accountant validates everything
  • Nothing leaves without the user: the Gmail recap is a draft, the Drive filing is announced, no data flows to an MCP that wasn't detected and named
  • A pack with missing pieces is never presented as complete — the missing list leads the letter
  • Masked IBANs (last 4 digits) · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere · 403 on list_cash_flow_categories → label fallback
