## 🔔 Transaction-triggered nudges

### Reverse-charge (autoliquidação) on intra-EU B2B services — ALWAYS applies
**Universal EU rule — even under Art. 53 VAT exemption.** ✅ When invoicing B2B services to a business customer in another EU member state:
- Issue invoice **without VAT** ✅
- Mark invoice: **"IVA — Autoliquidação"** + cite legal reference (reverse-charge applies under CIVA) ✅
- Include customer's NIF (EU tax ID) ✅
- Customer (the EU business) settles VAT in their own country ✅
- You must declare the autoliquidação in your VAT return (periodic declaration, quarterly if turnover < €650,000) ✅
- **Critical:** ✅ Reverse-charge applies even if you are VAT-exempt under art. 53; you cannot avoid the declaration

### IVA Exemption (art. 53 CIVA) — threshold & restrictions
- **Annual turnover limit (previous calendar year):** ✅ €15,000 in Portugal (https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/artigo-53-o-do-civa.aspx)
- **Safety margin during current year:** ✅ If you exceed €18,750 (€15,000 + 25%) in current year, you must notify tax authority within 15 business days (Portal das Finanças)
- **Scope:** Applies to Portuguese turnover (domestic services & goods sales) ✅
- **Exclusions from exemption:** Taxpayers performing only one taxable operation, intra-community transport of new vehicles ✅
- **If exempt:** You cannot deduct input VAT on business purchases; you cannot recover VAT paid ✅
- **Invoices must state:** No VAT (or state the exemption basis: art. 53 CIVA) + your NIF + customer name/NIF ✅
- **Automatic application:** No prior approval needed if < €15,000 previous year ✅
- **Transition rule (2025):** ✅ If you exceeded €15,000 in 2024, you exited exemption Jan 1, 2025 (Portal das Finanças)
