## 🔔 Transaction-triggered nudges

### Reverse-charge / Autoliquidazione on intra-EU B2B services

Even under the forfettario VAT exemption (RF19), **reverse-charge (autoliquidazione) applies to purchases of services from EU/extra-EU suppliers**:

- **Mechanism:** Forfettari receiving invoices from foreign B2B service providers must issue an **autofattura** (self-invoice) applying reverse-charge
- **VAT treatment:** No VAT charged to client; reverse-charge calculated on the supplier invoice amount (at 22% standard or applicable rate)
- **Declaration & payment:** As of **Oct 1, 2025** ✅, IVA from reverse-charge is versed on a **quarterly basis** (not monthly) by the **16th of the second month following each quarter** ✅ (e.g., April 16 for Q1; July 16 for Q2; Oct 16 for Q3; Jan 16 for Q4). *Source: [Commercialista Telematico](https://www.commercialistatelematico.com/articoli/2025/06/iva-e-reverse-charge-da-1-ottobre-2025-versamento-trimestrale-anche-per-forfettari.html)*
- **Intra-EU acquisitions >€10k:** Integrate the invoice with the reverse-charge details within prescribed timelines
- **Documentation:** Keep the supplier invoice + self-issued autofattura (F2 document type or TD18 codice documento) in records

### E-fattura (Electronic Invoice) — RF19 Regime, Mandatory

**All invoices for supplies to customers in Italy must be transmitted electronically since Jan 1, 2024:**

- **Format:** XML via Sistema di Interscambio (SDI)
- **Regime code:** RF19 (Regime forfettario, Art. 1 c. 54-89, Law 190/2014)
- **VAT natura code:** N2.2 ("Operazioni non soggette – altri casi" / Non-subject operations—other cases)
- **VAT rate:** 0%
- **Mandatory diciture (statement):** "Operazione senza applicazione dell'IVA, effettuata ai sensi dell'articolo 1, commi da 54 a 89, L. n. 190 del 2014 così come modificato dalla L. n. 208 del 2015 e dalla L. n. 145 del 2018"
- **Bollo virtuale (stamp duty):** "SI" if invoice amount > €77.47 ✅ (€2 stamp deducted/reported) ✅
- **Filing deadline:** Immediate invoices within 12 days; consolidated invoices by the 15th of the following month

### **Zero VAT charged, but deduction prohibited**

- Forfettari do **not** charge VAT to clients (RF19 status)
- Forfettari do **not** deduct VAT paid on purchases (unlike ordinary regime)
- Exception: reverse-charge VAT on foreign B2B services must be reported and versed quarterly (see above)

### Mileage, meals, equipment, and other expense deductions

**No deduction allowed.** The forfettario regime operates on a **flat-rate (forfait) model**, not actual expense deduction:

- The **profitability coefficient** (40%–86% depending on ATECO) replaces all expense deduction
- Individual deductions for vehicle mileage, meal allowances, supplies, equipment, software, professional subscriptions, etc., **do not apply**
- The coefficient amount not included in the taxable base implicitly accounts for typical business costs
- Only **mandatory INPS social contributions** reduce the calculated taxable income before applying the 15% or 5% flat tax

### Invoice-to-cash: no deduction without e-fattura

- Electronically transmitted invoices (via SDI) serve as proof of income for tax purposes
- Invoices must contain RFC (Responsabile della Fatturazione Elettronica) designation if outsourced
- Missing or delayed e-fattura transmission may trigger compliance penalties (€100–€1,000 per missing invoice) or loss of regime eligibility

## 🎯 Reliefs & niches to activate (regime-specific)

### 5% reduced imposta sostitutiva (startup rate)

- **Eligibility:** First **five years** of activity, provided the individual did **not** exercise any professional, artisanal, or business activity (including family/partnership forms) in the **three years preceding** regime entry
- **Calculation:** Same coefficient-based formula, but tax rate = 5% instead of 15%
- **Example:** Reddito imponibile €15,000; tax under 5% = €750 vs. 15% = €2,250
- **Verification:** Declare lack of prior activity in regime entry documentation; maintain records of start date

### Coefficient-based calculation eliminates actual-cost tracking

- No need to track receipts for office supplies, software, tools, phone bills, utilities, professional development, etc.
- The ATECO-specific coefficient (e.g., 78% for professional services) implicitly budgets ~78% revenue as taxable; remaining ~22% deemed as costs
- Simplifies accounting and reduces audit risk vs. ordinary regime (where cost substantiation is mandatory)

### INPS contributions are deductible

- Mandatory INPS contributions (gestione separata or artigiani/commercianti) **reduce the calculated taxable income** before applying the flat tax rate
- **Formula:** Taxable Income = (Gross Revenue × Coefficient) − Mandatory INPS Contributions
- Then: Tax = Taxable Income × 15% (or 5%)
- Reduces overall tax burden and aligns with ordinary-regime treatment of social contributions

### No personal/family deductions

- Unlike IRPEF (personal income tax), the forfettario flat-tax **does not allow** deductions for:
  - Mortgage interest
  - Medical/healthcare expenses
  - Family dependents (figli a carico)
  - Life insurance or pension contributions
- If the individual has *other* income streams subject to IRPEF (e.g., employment, rental), family deductions may apply to those income sources separately

## 📅 2026 Thresholds & deadlines

### Annual turnover ceiling & exit triggers

| Threshold | Consequence |
|-----------|-------------|
| **€85,000 (annual revenue limit)** ✅ | Regime cap; must not be exceeded |
| €85,001–€100,000 ✅ | Exit effective **January 1 of following year** (2027) |
| >€100,000 ✅ | **Immediate exit mid-year** to ordinary regime (pro-rata taxation) |

*Source: Law 190/2014, c. 54-89; confirmed via [Agenzia delle Entrate Risposte 2026](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/20143/9761612/Risposta+n.+68_2026/2762d55b-1fea-d3e6-6b0b-76c0b65ecdca)*

### Dependent employment income allowance (2026 transitional) ✅

- Up to **€35,000** in parallel employee/subordinate income permitted without regime loss (2026 only; reverts to €30,000 from 2027)
- Exceeding €35,000 triggers exclusion from forfettario
- *Source: [Fiscomania 2026 Forfettario](https://fiscomania.com/forfettario-e-lavoro-dipendente/)*

### Staff/contractor expense cap ✅

- **€20,000** annual ceiling on payroll, contractors, or subcontracted services
- Exceeding €20,000 results in loss of regime eligibility
- *Source: [Agenzia delle Entrate Requisiti](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/schede/agevolazioni/regime-agevolato-forfettario/requisiti-nuovo-regime-forfettario-agevolato)*

### Imposta sostitutiva (flat tax) rates ✅

| Rate | Conditions |
|------|-----------|
| **5%** ✅ | First 5 years; no prior business/professional/artisan activity in preceding 3 years |
| **15%** ✅ | Standard rate (after 5 years or if startup conditions not met) |

*Replaces IRPEF + regional/municipal surtax (addizionali) + IRAP. Confirmed via [Agenzia delle Entrate](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it)*
