## 🔔 Transaction-triggered nudges

### Reverse-charge / self-accounting (VAT on foreign B2B services)
- **Universal rule**: ✅ When a non-German business supplies B2B services to your GmbH, VAT liability shifts to you as the recipient (§ 13b UStG, even if you are VAT-exempt as Kleinunternehmer). Confirmed: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__13b.html
- **Mechanism**: Invoice must NOT show itemized VAT; supplier states "Reverse charge – customer liable" with reference to § 13b UStG.
- **Your action**: Record the service as input AND output VAT simultaneously on your monthly/quarterly VAT return (Umsatzsteuererklärung). If VAT-exempt (Kleinunternehmer), you *cannot* recover the VAT but must still report it.
- **Declaration**: File on Form Umsatzsteuer-Voranmeldung (monthly or quarterly, depending on liability).
- **E-invoice requirement**: Cross-border reverse-charge invoices must be issued in EN 16931-compliant format (XRechnung, ZUGFeRD ≥2.0.1, or Peppol BIS 3.0) as of 1 Jan 2025.

### Invoice required to deduct / justify
- **General rule**: Every deductible business expense requires a contemporaneous, itemized invoice or receipt addressed to the GmbH, showing supplier name, date, description, amount, and VAT (if applicable).
- **E-invoice mandate (domestic B2B)**: 
  - **From 1 Jan 2025**: Ability to receive structured e-invoices is mandatory.
  - **From 1 Jan 2025 – 31 Dec 2026**: Paper/PDF invoices are allowed *only with recipient consent*; structured e-invoices preferred.
  - **From 1 Jan 2027**: If turnover > €800,000, you *must* issue e-invoices to other businesses in Germany (XRechnung, ZUGFeRD, or Peppol BIS 3.0).
  - **From 1 Jan 2028**: All GmbH businesses must issue e-invoices domestically (except Kleinunternehmer in issuance).
- **Retention**: Keep all invoices and supporting documents for **8 years** (§ 14b UStG).

### Mileage / business meals / equipment — FULLY DEDUCTIBLE
- **Mileage allowance**: ✅ €0.30 per km (unchanged 2026) for business travel (Geschäftsfahrten). GmbH-owned vehicles book actual mileage multiplied by this rate, or claim actual documented costs (fuel, maintenance, insurance, depreciation). Confirmed: https://ao.bundesfinanzministerium.de/lsth/2026/B-Anhaenge/Anhang-14/inhalt.html
- **Business meal allowances** (per diem, Verpflegungsmehraufwand): ✅ Confirmed for 2026 per BMF (https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Lohnsteuer/2025-12-05-steuerliche-behandlung-reisekosten-2026.pdf)
  - **8–24 hour absence**: ✅ €14 (no receipt required, flat claim).
  - **Multi-day trip**: ✅ €28/day (no receipt required).
  - **Day of departure/return**: ✅ €14 each (if traveling).
  - **Hotel breakfast rule**: ✅ If breakfast not itemized separately on hotel bill, deduct €5.60/day from hotel cost (already covered by meal allowance).
  - If meals provided by company or at company expense, reduce per diem accordingly.
- **Equipment & materials**: Fully deductible if purchased for business purposes. Requires invoice to GmbH. Items under €150 (net) can be expensed immediately; over €150 must be capitalized and depreciated (straight-line or accelerated depreciation options available).
- **Home office**: If a dedicated room is used ≥90% for work, deduct actual proportional rent, utilities, heating, internet.

### Geschäftsführer salary — must avoid verdeckte Gewinnausschüttung (hidden profit distribution)
- **Deductibility rule**: A Geschäftsführer salary is a business expense *if and only if it is "angemessen" (reasonable/market-level)*.
- **What triggers a hidden profit distribution (vGA)**:
  - Salary is excessive compared to foreign/arm's-length salaries for equivalent roles in comparable companies.
  - Formal errors: tantiem (variable profit-share) agreement made *retroactively* (must be in writing *before* fiscal year starts).
  - Bonus ratio unreasonable: rule of thumb is ≥75% fixed salary, ≤25% variable tantiem.
- **Tax consequence**: Excess portion is added back to taxable income; does NOT reduce the GmbH's Körperschaftsteuer base.
- **Assessment criteria**: Company size, profitability, industry, Geschäftsführer's tasks, foreign salary comparables.
- **Record requirement**: Any tantiem agreement must be documented in writing before the fiscal year begins.

## 🎯 Reliefs / niches to activate

### Research Allowance (Forschungszulage / R&D tax credit) — SMEs
- **Eligibility**: ✅ All GmbH businesses (and all legal forms) can claim this, regardless of profitability or whether they owe tax. (https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fzulg/)
- **Rate for SMEs**: ✅ 35% of eligible R&D costs (25% base rate + 10% SME bonus); large enterprises: 25%.
- **Eligible costs**: ✅ Personnel costs, external contract research, and (new from 2026) a flat 20% overhead deduction on other operating costs.
- **Maximum eligible expenditure**: ✅ €12 million/year (increased from €10M in 2025 for 2026-2030 period).
- **Application**: File form Forschungszulage-Zusatzbescheinigung with your corporate tax return or separately; does not require prior approval.
- **Impact**: Direct tax credit; can be carried forward or used to offset zero-tax-liability years.
- **Link**: ✅ https://www.gtai.de/en/invest/investment-guide/incentive-programs-in-germany/tax-support-for-innovation-and-growth-1915978

### Loss carryforward (Verlustvortrag)
- **Rule**: ✅ Losses can be carried forward indefinitely to offset future profits.
- **Limit (from 2027)**: ✅ Only €1 million of total loss carryforward is unrestricted; excess above €1M is usable only up to 70% of annual profit (subject to anti-abuse provisions). Confirmed: § 10d EStG (https://ao.bundesfinanzministerium.de/lsth/2026/A-Einkommensteuergesetz/II-Einkommen-2-24b/5-Sonderausgaben-10-10g/Paragraf-10d/inhalt.html)
- **Applies immediately**: ✅ 2026 and prior years remain under previous rules.

### Interest deduction limitations (Zinsschrankenregel)
- **General rule**: ✅ Interest expenses are deductible, but limited to 30% of tax EBITDA if interest expense exceeds €3 million annually. Confirmed: § 4h EStG / § 8a KStG (https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__4h.html)
- **Below €3M threshold**: ✅ No limitation; deduct all interest.
- **Applies to**: ⚠️ to verify: External debt, implicit financing costs in leasing/rental/royalties (25% of amounts >€200,000 added back).

### Gewerbesteuer credit (Anrechnung)
- **Mechanism**: Trade tax paid is credited 3.8% against corporate income tax liability (Anrechnung nach § 35 EStG / § 10a KStG). This partially offsets the combined burden.

## ⚠️ To verify

- **Municipal Gewerbesteuer multipliers (Hebesatz)**: Vary by Gemeinde; confirm your exact location's rate (range 250–580%, with average effective rate 14–17%). Some municipalities offer targeted reductions for startups or R&D-intensive businesses.
- **Church tax (Kirchensteuer)**: If Geschäftsführer or shareholder is church member, add 8–9% to income tax (for individuals); does not apply directly to corporate level.
- **Withholding on dividends**: 26.375% (25% + 5.5% SolZ); confirm if shareholder has exemptions (e.g., Teileinkünfteverfahren for ≥1% owners, or treaty exemptions for foreign shareholders).
- **Local trade tax allowances (Gewerbefreibetrag)**: Some municipalities grant small allowances (up to €24,500 in a few regions); check with your local Finanzamt or Gemeinde.
- **E-invoice SPF compliance**: Confirm your accounting software supports ZUGFeRD 2.0.1+ or Peppol BIS 3.0 before 1 Jan 2027.
- **Contract documentation for Geschäftsführer tantiem**: Ensure written agreement is dated *before* fiscal year starts and reflects market-level compensation to avoid vGA assessment disputes.
