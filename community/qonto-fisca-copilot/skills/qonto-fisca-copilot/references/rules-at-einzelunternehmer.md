## 🔔 Transaction-triggered nudges

### Reverse charge (Autoliquidation) on foreign B2B services
- **Applies universally**: Even Kleinunternehmer under VAT exemption must self-account for VAT on services received from non-Austrian enterprises. ✅ (confirmed: https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/umsatzsteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-zur-umsatzsteuer/umsaetze-mit-auslandsbezug/reverse-charge.html)
- **Mechanism**: Foreign supplier issues invoice net (no VAT); recipient calculates & declares VAT to Finanzamt (via UVA or annual return if exempt).
- **Invoice requirement**: Invoice must state "Reverse charge — Article 196 EUMD" + both parties' UID numbers; issued by 15th of following month. ✅ (confirmed: Article 196 EUMD and 15-day deadline)
- **Caveat**: Only if recipient holds an Austrian UID number; does not apply on certain services (land rental, toll fees, events).
- **Source**: [usp.gv.at reverse-charge](https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/umsatzsteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-zur-umsatzsteuer/umsaetze-mit-auslandsbezug/reverse-charge.html)

### Invoice VAT-exemption statement (Kleinunternehmer only)
- **Mandatory text on every invoice**: "Umsatzsteuerbefreit — Kleinunternehmer gemäß § 6 (1) Z 27 UStG"  
- **Consequence of error**: If VAT is mistakenly charged, the amount is owed to Finanzamt regardless of exemption eligibility.
- **Non-filing requirement**: No VAT returns (UVA/Jahreserklärung) due if under threshold. ✅
- **Source**: [usp.gv.at Kleinunternehmen](https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/umsatzsteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-zur-umsatzsteuer/weitere-steuertatbestaende-und-befreiungen/kleinunternehmen.html)

### Betriebsausgaben (business expenses)
- **Low-value assets (Geringwertiges Wirtschaftsgut)**: ≤€1,000 (excl. VAT if deductible) → full expense in acquisition year. ✅ (confirmed since Jan 1, 2023)
- **Motor vehicles**: Depreciation capped at €40,000 acquisition cost; useful life typically 8 years for cars/station wagons (straight-line or declining-balance). ✅
- **Business meals**: Only 50% deductible (exceptions: marketing events with documented concept are fully deductible). ✅
- **Documentation**: ⚠️ to verify — File claimed ">€50" threshold; official sources state ALL business expenses require documentation (Belegnachweis) regardless of amount. No €50 threshold exists in law. Exception: self-generated receipts (Eigenbelege) only when external documentation unavailable (tips, private sales).
- **Choice**: Either actual expenses OR flat-rate allowance (Pauschale), not both.
- **Source**: [usp.gv.at Belegnachweis](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuerliche-gewinnermittlung/weitere-informationen-zur-steuerlichen-gewinnermittlung/betriebseinnahmen-und-ausgaben/belegnachweis.html)

### E-invoicing & archiving
- **B2G mandate only**: Electronic invoicing required for public-sector suppliers (federal agencies). ✅ (in place since Jan 1, 2014)
- **No B2B/B2C mandate**: Paper invoices acceptable for private customers. ✅
- **Archiving**: All invoices/records retained 7 years (digital storage acceptable). ✅
- **Source**: [usp.gv.at e-invoicing](https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/umsatzsteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-zur-umsatzsteuer/vorsteuerabzug-und-rechnung/e-rechnung.html)

## 🎯 Reliefs / niches to activate

### Gewinnfreibetrag (profit deduction)
- **Automatic (no investment required)**: 15% of profit up to €33,000 (max €4,950). ✅
- **Investment-triggered**: Above €33,000, add 13% (next €145k), 7% (next €175k), 4.5% (next €230k) — requires fixed-asset acquisition same year. ✅
- **Maximum**: €46,400 total (applies at €583k+ profit). ✅
- **Benefit**: Reduces both income tax & socially-insurable profit (SVS base). ✅
- **Condition**: Tangible depreciable assets, min 4-year life; sold before 4 years = recapture tax. ✅
- **Source**: [usp.gv.at Gewinnfreibetrag](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuerliche-gewinnermittlung/weitere-informationen-zur-steuerlichen-gewinnermittlung/betriebseinnahmen-und-ausgaben/gewinnfreibetrag.html)

### Temporary doubled investment allowance (Nov 2025–Dec 2026)
- **Investitionsfreibetrag (IFB)**: Raised from 10% to 20% on tangible assets acquired in this window. ✅
- **Eco-investments (Öko-IFB)**: Raised from 15% to 22% (renewables, efficiency improvements). ✅
- **Declines** to normal rates in 2027. ✅
- **Source**: [usp.gv.at Investitionsfreibetrag](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuerliche-gewinnermittlung/weitere-informationen-zur-steuerlichen-gewinnermittlung/betriebseinnahmen-und-ausgaben/investitionsfreibetrag.html)
