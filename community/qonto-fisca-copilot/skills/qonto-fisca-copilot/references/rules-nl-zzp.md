## 🔔 Transaction-triggered nudges

### Reverse-charge / autoliquidatie on intra-EU B2B services (UNIVERSAL)
- **When it applies:** You supply services to a VAT-registered business (customer) in another EU member state → you do NOT charge VAT on the invoice; write "**VAT reverse-charged**" (Dutch: *btw verlegd*).
- **Your declaration:** You must declare this service in your quarterly **VAT return** (ICP / Intracommunautaire Prestaties section). ICP declaration due **2 months after end of period**.
- **VAT filing:** Quarterly on 30 April, 31 July, 31 October, 31 January.
- **IMPORTANT:** Reverse-charge applies **even if you use the KOR small-business VAT exemption** (€20k ceiling), BUT if you ARE under KOR, you cannot deduct VAT, so reverse-charge is a pass-through for your customer only; you still don't file a VAT return.

### Invoice requirement & e-invoicing (B2B mandatory 1 July 2030)
- **Now (2026):** No domestic B2B e-invoice mandate. Supplier to government (B2G) must use Peppol/Digipoort since 2019.
- **From 1 July 2030:** Cross-border B2B intra-EU services → **EN 16931 e-invoice (Peppol)** mandatory.
- **Invoice must show:** Your VAT ID, customer VAT ID, reverse-charge statement if applicable, date, amounts.

### Mileage (zakelijk kilometers) — DEDUCTIBLE
- **Rate:** ✅ **€0.25/km** (effective 2026, retroactive to 1 January 2026) [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/zakelijk-gebruik-privevervoermiddel-2026]
- **What counts:** Business trips only (not commute to your own office).
- **Documentation:** Date, odometer start/end, addresses, business purpose.
- **Limit:** Deductible in full; no additional fuel/maintenance deduction.

### Business meals & representation (representatiekosten) — PARTIALLY DEDUCTIBLE
- **Rule:** ✅ Either deduct **80%** of cost, OR deduct amounts **above €5,700 annual threshold** (2026) [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/drempel-beperkt-aftrekbare-kosten-2026]
- **Covers:** Business lunches/dinners, gifts (food/beverages), conferences, seminars, study trips.
- **VAT trap:** VAT on representation costs is **NOT deductible** (even if business-related).

### Equipment & tools
- **Deductible:** Office equipment, software, hardware, professional tools.
- **NOT deductible:** Personal clothes, fines, own mobile devices.

### 2. MKB-winstvrijstelling (SME profit exemption) — 12.7% (2026)
- **What it is:** ✅ A deduction from taxable profit = **12.7% of profit (after zelfstandigenaftrek applied)** [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/mkb-winstvrijstelling-2026]
- **Tax benefit:** ✅ Calculated at **37.56% rate** (not your marginal rate), so actual saving is ~4.8% of profit [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/mkb-winstvrijstelling-2026]
- **Conditions:**
  - ✅ You are self-employed in Box 1 [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling]
  - ✅ No profit → exemption worsens your position (reduces fiscal loss) [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling]
  - ✅ Does NOT apply to income as co-owner/creditor in another business [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling]
- **Phasing:** ✅ 12.7% in 2025–2026; was 13.31% in 2024 [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/mkb-winstvrijstelling-2026]. Will be phased down further (not confirmed for 2027).
- **Source:** [Belastingdienst MKB-winstvrijstelling 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/mkb_winstvrijstelling)

### 4. KOR (Kleineondernemersregeling) — Small Business VAT Exemption
- **Threshold:** ✅ ≤€20,000 annual turnover (both current & previous year) [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden]

- **Tradeoff:** ✅ Cannot reclaim input VAT on costs/investments [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden]
- **Registration:** ⚠️ to verify — Claim: "Must register by 3 December previous year for 1 January eligibility." Not confirmed in the specific sources; official page says "submit only after receiving documentation from tax authority."
- **Reverse-charge on intra-EU services:** ✅ STILL applies even under KOR (you do not charge, customer reverse-charges), but you don't file VAT return unless exceeding threshold [https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden]
- **Source:** [Belastingdienst KOR conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/kor-voorwaarden)
