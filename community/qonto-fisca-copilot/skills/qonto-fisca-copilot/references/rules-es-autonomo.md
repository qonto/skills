## 🔔 Transaction-triggered nudges

### Reverse-charge (Inversión del Sujeto Pasivo) on intra-EU B2B services — **MANDATORY** ✅
- **Scope**: All B2B invoices to registered VAT payers in other EU member states must apply reverse-charge (0% Spanish IVA). ✅
- **Condition**: Invoice recipient must have valid NIF-IVA in VIES registry. If no valid number, invoice at standard 21% VAT. ✅
- **Required wording on invoice**: Include "Inversión del Sujeto Pasivo (Reverse Charge) – Article 196 EU VAT Directive 2006/112/EC." ✅
- **ROI registration**: Mandatory to register in ROI (Registro de Operadores Intracomunitarios) to invoice without IVA to EU companies. ✅
- **Declaration**: Report in Modelo 303 (quarterly VAT form), box 61 ("Operations not subject or reverse-charged"). ✅
- **Note**: Applies even if autónomo is under small-business VAT exemption (when/if threshold is introduced). ✅

### Invoice required to deduct & justify expenses
- **Documentation**: Every deductible expense requires a document (invoice, receipt, bank statement) showing seller, buyer, date, description, amount, and VAT.
- **Retention**: Keep for four years.
- **Quarterly reporting**: Modelo 130 (IRPF advance) filed quarterly (April 20, July 20, October 20, January 30); supports Modelo 100 final return.

### Verifactu e-invoicing mandate (timeline) ✅
- **Deadline for autónomos**: July 1, **2027** (not 2026; postponed). ✅ (https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/sistemas-informaticos-facturacion-verifactu/nota-informativa-ampliacion-plazo-adaptacion-facturacion.html)
- **Requirement**: All invoices issued to Spanish VAT-registered customers must use AEAT-certified invoicing software with digital signature & QR code.
- **Non-compliant software penalty**: Up to €50,000/year; €10,000 per non-compliant B2B invoice.
- **Exemption**: Autónomos already reporting via SII (Immediate Supply of Information) system are generally exempt.

### Business meals & mileage
- **Meals**: Only deductible if away from office on work trip (client visit, conference, supplier meeting). Cap: **€26.67/day** ✅ (https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/en_gb/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-ayuda-presentacion/irpf-2021/7-cumplimentacion-irpf/7_1-rendimientos-trabajo-personal/7_1_1-rendimientos-integros/7_1_1_2-dietas-gastos-viaje/asignaciones-gastos-manutencion-estancia.html), card payment required.
- **Mileage**: Personal vehicle use up to 50% deductible (requires adviser approval); business-registered vehicle fuel/repairs/tolls 100% deductible.
- **Both regimes**: Deductible under **Estimación Directa** (direct assessment). Under módulos (forfait regime), use flat-rate expense percentages instead.

## 🎯 Reliefs / niches to activate

### RETA income-based brackets (new system since Jan 2023)
**15-tier system**: Quota tied to actual monthly net income, not a flat choice. Monthly net income determines quarterly or annual bracket shift (allowed up to 6 changes/year, effective every 2 months).

| Bracket | Monthly Net Income | Monthly Quota |
|---------|-------------------|---------------|
| 1 | ≤ €670 | € 200 |
| 2 | € 670–900 | € 220 |
| 3 | € 900–1,167 | € 260 |
| 4 | € 1,167–1,300 | € 290 |
| 5 | € 1,300–1,500 | € 294 |
| 6 | € 1,500–1,700 | € 294 |
| 7 | € 1,700–1,850 | € 310 |
| 8 | € 1,850–2,030 | € 315 |

**2026 update**: Quotas frozen vs. 2025; only MEI (professional accident surcharge) increased by 0.1% to 0.9%. ✅ (https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-7296)

### Difficult-to-justify expenses (Gastos de Difícil Justificación) ✅
- **Automatic deduction**: 5% of annual net income from activities. ✅
- **Annual ceiling**: € 2,000 max deduction. ✅
- **Available to**: Autónomos under **Estimación Directa Simplificada** (simplified direct assessment, most common regime). ✅
- **No receipt required**, but deduction is automatic—claim in Modelo 100. ✅
(https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/irpf/empresarios-individuales-profesionales/regimenes-determinar-rendimiento-actividad/estimacion-directa-simplificada.html)

### Recargo de Equivalencia (Equivalence Surcharge) VAT regime ✅
- **Scope**: Mandatory for retail autónomos selling > 80% to end consumers without manufacturing.
- **Surcharge rates**: 5.2% (on 21% VAT products), 1.4% (on 10%), 0.5% (on 4%). ✅ (https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/regimenes-tributacion-iva/regimen-especial-recargo-equivalencia/que-consiste-regimen-especial-recargo-equivalencia.html)
- **Benefit**: Supplier adds surcharge to invoice; autónomo does not file Modelo 303 (VAT form), no VAT input recovery allowed.
- **Simplified compliance**: Only file Modelo 303 for imports, intra-community purchases, or reverse-charged services.

### Professional expense deductions (Estimación Directa)
- **Social security contributions (RETA)**: 100% deductible.
- **Home office/rent**: 10–50% deductible (based on % of professional use).
- **Professional services** (lawyers, accountants, bookkeepers): 100% deductible.
- **Training/courses** related to business: 100% deductible.
- **Software/subscriptions** (Adobe, CRM, accounting tools): 100% deductible.
- **Travel on business trips**: 100% deductible.
- **Professional liability & D&O insurance**: 100% deductible; ⚠️ to verify: life insurance up to €500/year (not confirmed in official sources).
- **Vehicle**: Personal car 50% max; business-registered 100%.
