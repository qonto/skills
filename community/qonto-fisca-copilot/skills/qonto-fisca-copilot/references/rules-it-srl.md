## 🔔 Transaction-triggered nudges

### Reverse-charge / autoliquidazione VAT on B2B intra-EU services

**Universal rule:** applies to SRL even with no VAT exemption. When you purchase B2B services from an EU supplier WITHOUT Italian VAT (not VAT-registered in Italy), **you** must self-invoice and settle Italian VAT.

- **Mechanism (autoliquidazione):** Issue a self-invoice (autofattura) with document code **TD17** ⚠️ to verify "within 12 days" — standard practice is by 15th of following month
- **VAT rate:** ✅ Apply 22% Italian VAT on invoice amount (https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/schede/comunicazioni/fatture-e-corrispettivi/faq-fe/risposte-alle-domande-piu-frequenti-categoria/emissione-delle-fatture-elettroniche)
- **VAT effect:** ✅ Register 22% as *debit* on sales register AND *credit* on purchase register → net zero VAT liability for period, but must report in IVA declaration
- **E-invoicing:** ✅ Transmit self-invoice to SdI (Sistema di Interscambio) by the **15th of the month following** service month (https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/schede/comunicazioni/fatture-e-corrispettivi/faq-fe/risposte-alle-domande-piu-frequenti-categoria/modalita-di-trasmissione-delle-fatture-elettroniche)
- **Penalty for omission:** ✗→ €500–€20,000 if no self-invoice issued (90% of VAT not issued, min €500, not €250–€2,000) (https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/20143/5612116/Risposta+n.+450_2023.pdf); 70% of unpaid VAT if affecting IVA liquidation
- **Foreign VAT recovery:** If supplier applies foreign VAT (e.g., German 19%), that cost is NOT recoverable in Italy; pursue EU VAT refund portal by **30 Sept** following year (min. threshold €400)

### Amministratore compenso (director compensation)
- **Deduction for IRES only:** ✅ €1 of net compenso reduces IRES taxable income by €1 → €0.24 IRES saving; NOT deductible for IRAP (https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/dichiarazione-irap-2026/che-cos-)
- **Requisites:** ✅ Must be formally deliberated by shareholder assembly or stated in bylaws; amount, frequency, and name specified in resolution
- **Cash-basis rule:** ✅ Compenso deductible in tax year **paid** (not accrued); payment by **12 January** of following year counts as prior-year deduction
- **Personal tax burden on amministratore:**
  - IRPEF (progressive): 23–43% + regional/municipal surtax (1–4%)
  - INPS Gestione Separata: ✅ **35.03%** if no other mandatory pension; **24%** if already pension-insured (https://www.inps.it/it/it/inps-comunica/notizie/dettaglio-news-page.news.2026.02.gestione-separata-le-aliquote-contributive-per-il-2026.html)
  - Example: €80,000 gross → ~€46,663 net (net burden >40%)
- **INPS contribution split:** ✅ 2/3 borne by company, 1/3 deducted from administrator's compensation

### Dividends (distribution of profits)
- **Withholding tax:** ✅ 26% definitive withholding on all profits distributed to individual shareholders (profits from 2018 onwards) (https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/20143/8703578/Risposta+n.+59_2025/ac1e518a-9ec4-7b4e-bb4a-95de036507fb)
- **Shareholder treatment:** ✅ Receives dividend net of 26%; NO additional IRPEF reporting required (closes tax position)
- **Company obligation:** ✅ Apply withholding at payment; remit by **16th of month following each quarter** (quarterly basis); issue Certificazione Unica (CU) by **16 March** following year (https://www1.agenziaentrate.gov.it/servizi/scadenzario/main.php?op=4&chi=3812&cosa=10864&come=515&entroil=16-04-2025)
- **Trade-off vs. compenso:** ✅ For marginal IRPEF >50%, dividends (26%) often more efficient than compenso; verify with accountant

## 🎯 Reliefs / niches to activate

### IRES Premiale (reduced rate to 20%)
- **Eligibility (2025 tax year only; unclear if extended to 2026):** ✅ Confirmed for 2025 — requires accrual of ≥80% of 2024 profits to reserve + qualifying investments in Industry 4.0/5.0 assets ≥30% of accrued profits + maintenance of workforce + specific hiring requirements (https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/risposte-alle-domande-pi%C3%B9-frequenti5)
- **Rate:** ✅ 20% instead of ordinary 24% (2025 filing in 2026)
- **Status:** ⚠️ to verify — Confirmed for FY 2025; **not confirmed for 2026 under current law** — verify before planning (https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/20143/8405066/071_Com.st.+Modelli+dichiarativi+2026+19.12.2026)

### Deductible expenses (ordinary SRL)
- **Fully deductible:** ✅ Personnel costs (gross wages, overtime, bonuses, INPS), professional fees (legal, tax, consulting), materials & supplies, facility rent, utilities, equipment maintenance, training courses, bookkeeping/accounting
- **Partially deductible:** ✅ Telephone/internet 80%, company vehicle (mixed use) 20%, vehicle (employee fringe) 70%, representative vehicle 80% (https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/20143/8410837/Circolare+n.+15+del+22_12_2025.pdf), entertainment & representation 1.5% of revenue (capped €10M) (https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/20143/8410837/Circolare+n.+15+del+22_12_2025.pdf), travel meals & lodging 75%
- **NOT deductible:** ✅ Fines & penalties, income taxes (IRES itself), illegal activity costs, unqualified charitable gifts
- **No flat-rate exemption for SRL:** ✅ Unlike natural persons with regime forfettario (€85k threshold), SRL must register VAT regardless of turnover and cannot claim flat-rate deduction simplifications

### SRLS (Società a Responsabilità Limitata Semplificata)
- **When it makes sense:** ✅ Minimal capital start-up (€1), family business, seasonal/short-term ventures with low growth; typically cost-inefficient for ongoing operations (same tax rates + rigid statute)
- **Tax rates:** ✅ Identical to standard SRL (IRES 24%, IRAP 3.9% base + regional variation, dividends 26%)
- **Constraint:** ✅ Standardized, non-modifiable statute → cannot implement tax-planning strategies available to ordinary SRL (e.g., profit-deferral structures, reinvestment schemes)
