## 🔔 Transaction-Triggered Nudges

### Reverse-Charge / Autoliquidação VAT on Foreign B2B Services
**Applies universally to Lda, even under VAT exemption (€15k threshold).**
- When receiving services from suppliers outside Portugal (EU or non-EU), the **Lda acts as the "buyer" and self-accounts for VAT**.
- Mechanism: Supplier issues invoice with **"IVA autoliquidação"** notation (no VAT charged); Lda records the reverse charge in VAT return (Modelo 360/Anexo) in same VAT period.
- **Monthly SAF-T (PT) Billing declaration required by the 5th of following month** ✅ (certified software via Autoridade Tributária e Aduaneira [AT]; https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/apoio_ao_contribuinte/Negocios/Faturacao/SAF_T_PT/Paginas/default.aspx).
- No impact on input VAT recovery — reverse-charged VAT is fully deductible if service is for business purpose.

### E-Invoicing & Documentation (Mandatory 2026)
- **All invoices must use AT-certified software** ✅ (number must appear on every invoice).
- **ATCUD (unique document code) and QR code required** ✅ on every invoice since January 1, 2023.
- **PDF invoices valid until December 31, 2026** ✅ (no Qualified Electronic Signature [QES] yet required); from January 1, 2027, QES becomes mandatory (https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/12/portugal-postponement-mandatory-qes-saft-requirements.html).
- **No invoice = no deduction:** Expenses without proper invoiced documentation are non-deductible; invoices must be issued in company name.
- Monthly SAF-T (PT) filing mandatory by 5th of following month. Annual SAF-T: ✅ First mandatory filing in 2028 for 2026 transactions (https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/12/portugal-postponement-mandatory-qes-saft-requirements.html).

### Manager Remuneration (Remuneração do Gerente)
- **Salary is deductible** as a business expense if:
  - Properly documented via payslips and employment contract.
  - Declared in personal IRS (Modelo 3) by the gerente.
  - Social security contributions (✅ 34.75% total) are withheld and paid to AT/Social Security (https://taxravens.com/en/portugal/social-security).
- **Withholding tax not required** on salary (unlike dividends); only social security applies.
- **If unpaid gerente:** Minimum social security contribution base is 1 IAS (✅ €537.13 in 2026; https://www.garrigues.com/en_GB/new/portugal-indexing-reference-social-benefits-updated-eu-53713-2026).

### Dividends / Lucros Distribuídos
- **Withholding tax (retenção na fonte) at 28%** ✅ on distributions to individual members (final tax or option to include in IRS; https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs71.aspx).
- **No withholding on distributions between companies** if participation exemption applies (Lda holds ≥10% of share capital for ≥1 year, both entities subject to IRC).
- Declared dividend distributions must appear on tax return; participation exemption must be documented.

## 🎯 Reliefs & Niches to Activate

### SME Reduced IRC Rate: First EUR 50,000
- **15% on first €50,000 of taxable income** (vs. 19% general rate).
- Excess taxable income taxed at 19%.
- **Applies to Lda if classified as PME:** typical criteria include <250 employees, annual revenue <€50M, and independence ownership (not controlled by larger entity).
- **Lda is eligible automatically** if registered as PME with Portuguese tax authority.

### Depreciation & Amortization (Dedução por Depreciação)
- Equipment, vehicles, IT, and real estate used for business are **deductible via depreciation schedules**.
- Depreciation rates follow AT tables (e.g., computers 33.3% per year, vehicles 20% per year, buildings 2% per year).
- Land is **not depreciable**.
- Requires proper capitalization and accounting records.

### Participation Exemption (Exemção de Participações)
- **Dividends from other companies are tax-exempt at IRC level if:**
  - Lda holds ≥10% of share capital of the paying company.
  - Participation maintained continuously for ≥1 year.
  - Both companies subject to IRC (or equivalent) and not exempt.
- Must be claimed in tax return with supporting documentation (share certificate, acquisition proof).

### Deductible Expenses General
- **All expenses necessary to obtain or guarantee business income** are deductible (Article 23 of CIRC).
- Meals/entertainment: **must be invoiced to Lda, documented with date/purpose, and linked to business activity** (e.g., client meetings).
- Office supplies, utilities, rent, insurance, professional fees (accountant, lawyer) are deductible if properly documented.
- **No deduction without invoice** — vendor must issue invoice in company name.
