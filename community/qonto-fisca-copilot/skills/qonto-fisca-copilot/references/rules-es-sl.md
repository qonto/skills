## 🔔 Transaction-triggered nudges

### Reverse-charge VAT on foreign B2B services (Autoliquidación)
**Universal EU rule—applies even under an exemption; local mechanism in Spain:**
- **Receiving services from non-Spanish EU suppliers**: You (the SL) must self-assess the VAT on Form 303 (modelo 303).
  - **Example**: Consulting or software services from a France-based firm to your SL. You do NOT pay them VAT; instead, you declare the VAT on your 303 and deduct it as input VAT in the same period if the service is for the business.
  - **Declaration**: Reported on Modelo 303 (VAT self-assessment quarterly), with Form 349 (intra-community operations) filed by July 20 annually for the prior year.
  - **Source**: [Agencia Tributaria — Prestaciones de servicios](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/iva-operaciones-comercio-exterior/prestaciones-servicios.html)

### E-invoicing (VERI*FACTU) is mandatory for SL from Jan 1, 2027 ✗→ fixed
**All SL entities must comply:**
- **Requirement**: ✅ All invoices issued after Dec 31, 2026 must be electronic, must include a QR code, and must bear the reference "VERI*FACTU" or "Factura verificable en la sede electrónica de la AEAT."
- **Software**: Your accounting system must be certified to meet VERI*FACTU standards (integridad, trazabilidad, immutability).
- **Free public tool**: The Agencia Tributaria provides a free platform (Factura Plus) as backup if you lack certified software.
- **Penalty for non-compliance**: Up to €50,000 for using non-certified software; up to €10,000 for issuing non-electronic invoices.
- **Exception**: Simplified invoices under €300 (certain conditions) may be exempt; check current rules.
- **Source**: [Agencia Tributaria — Facturación electrónica obligatoria (Real Decreto 238/2026)](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-noticias/2026/marzo/31/facturacion-electronica-obligatoria.html)

### Invoice required to deduct expenses; retain all documentation
**Fundamental rule for Impuesto sobre Sociedades (IS):**
- **No invoice, no deduction**: Meals, travel, services, goods—you must have a valid Spanish invoice (with VAT number, date, amount, concept) or justified supporting documents to claim any deduction.
- **Meals & entertainment**: Are deductible (not subject to IRPF withholding on the company side), but you need itemized invoices. Business meals with clients/suppliers are "gastos de representación" (entertainment expenses), not "dietas" (meal allowances).
- **Travel & mileage**: No statutory mileage rate in Spain (unlike some EU countries). You deduct **actual costs**: fuel, tolls, parking, hotels. Must document with invoices/receipts.
- **Recent court ruling (STS 426/2025, STS 546/2025)**: The Supreme Court confirmed that **lack of formal documentation is the #1 reason Hacienda denies deductions on audit**. Even legal expenses must be invoiced to be deductible.
- **Administrator salary**: If paid as nómina (payroll), it is deductible only if the retribution is explicitly authorized in the company bylaws (estatutos sociales) OR the SL demonstrates that an actual service was rendered and the salary is reasonable. Court rulings now allow deduction even if salary is not in bylaws, provided real services are proven and expense is documented.
- **Source**: [Copilot Gestoría — Dietas 2026](https://copilotgestoria.com/blog/dietas-gastos-locomocion-2026-importes-exentos-irpf-errores-nomina-inspeccion-guia-gestorias); [Agencia Tributaria — Manual de Sociedades](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/manual-sociedades-2025/)

## 🎯 Reliefs & niches to activate

### New company reduced rate: 15% for first 2 profitable years
**Incentive for newly created SL entities:**
- **Rate**: 15% instead of 25% (or 19%/21% micro scale) in the **first two consecutive fiscal years in which the SL records a positive taxable base**.
- **Timing clock**: Starts from the first year with profit, not incorporation date. Example: if the SL was incorporated in 2023 but first turned a profit in 2025, it applies 15% in 2025 and 2026, then normal rates from 2027.
- **Conditions**:
  - The SL must not form part of a corporate group (grupo mercantil).
  - The activity must not have been exercised by a related person/entity in the year before incorporation (no business transfer).
  - The activity must be genuine (not a holding/patrimony company).
- **No carve-outs for distributing dividends**: The relief applies to taxable base before dividends; dividends are never deductible in IS.
- **Source**: [Agencia Tributaria — Tipo reducido 15% nueva creación](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/impuesto-sobre-sociedades/que-base-imponible-se-determina-sociedades/tipo-impositivo.html)

### Capitalization reserve reduction: ✅ 20–30% of base imponible
**Direct reduction of taxable base (not a deduction); new/expanded in 2026:**
- **Mechanism**: If the SL increases its equity (capital contributions, retained earnings capitalized, or share issues) during the fiscal year, it can reduce its taxable base by ✅ **20% of that increase** (up to 20% of positive taxable base) or ✅ **25%** if annual revenue < €1M.
- **Requirement**: The equity increase must be **maintained for 3 consecutive years** from the end of the year in which the reduction is claimed. If withdrawn early, Hacienda can recapture the reduction.
- **Accounting entry**: Must create a specific restricted reserve labeled "Capitalization Reserve, Article 25 LIS" in the balance sheet; cannot be distributed as dividends during the 3-year lockup.
- **Example**: SL with €100k profit and €40k equity increase → reduction of €8k (20% of €40k), bringing taxable base to €92k.
- **Source**: [Agencia Tributaria — Reducciones base imponible](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/impuesto-sobre-sociedades/que-base-imponible-se-determina-sociedades/reducciones-base-imponible.html)

### Accelerated amortization for electric vehicles & charging infrastructure (2026)
**Freedom of amortization (libertad de amortización) — no standard depreciation schedule required:**
- **EV vehicles**: ✅ New electric/hydrogen vehicles (BEV, FCHV, REEV, PHEV) acquired in 2026 can be fully depreciated in the year of purchase (100% deduction) or spread per company policy.
- **Charging infrastructure**: New charging stations (normal & high-power) installed and in service during 2026 can be fully amortized in 2026.
- **Requirement**: Must be "new" (first-time put into service by your SL), not used/refurbished.
- **Benefit duration**: Prorogued (extended) for periods starting in 2026 only; review 2027 legislation for renewal.
- **Source**: [Agencia Tributaria — Libertad amortización vehículos eléctricos (RD-ley 7/2026)](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/manual-sociedades-2025/capitulo-05-liquidacion-is-determinacion-imponible/bi-antes-reserva-capitalizac-compensac-00550/detalle-correcc-result-cuenta-perdidas-is/amortizaciones/libertad-amortizacion-determinados-vehiculos.html)

### Reduction of taxable base for income from intangible assets
**Available but rarely used by small SL:**
- The tax code allows a reduction for certain IP/patent licensing income under specific conditions (e.g., acquired intangibles, R&D investment). Rarely applies to typical service SL; check with gestor if relevant.
- **Source**: [Agencia Tributaria — Reducciones base imponible](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/impuesto-sobre-sociedades/que-base-imponible-se-determina-sociedades/reducciones-base-imponible.html)

### VAT rates (Impuesto sobre el Valor Añadido) — standard rates

| Rate | Applies to | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **✅ 21%** (general) | Most goods & services | Food (non-basic), alcohol, tech, professional services, hospitality, transport |
| **✅ 10%** (reduced) | Certain foods, hospitality, transport | Restaurant meals, food products (processed), passenger transport |
| **✅ 4%** (super-reduced) | Essential goods | Bread, milk, medications, olive oil, utilities (partial) |
| **✅ 0%** (zero) | Exports, certain services | EU B2B services (reverse-charge applies), food exports |

- **No IVA exemption threshold in Spain for domestic operations**: Unlike France (€85k) or other EU countries, Spain does not have a small-business IVA exemption (franquicia). All SL entities are in standard VAT regime unless explicitly exempted (e.g., real estate, certain services).
- **IVA franquicia legislative proposal**: A bill to introduce an €85k exemption threshold for small businesses passed parliament in March 2026 but was not yet in force as of July 2026; implementation pending regulatory details and expected delay.
- **Reverse-charge (autoliquidación) on EU B2B services**: When your SL receives a service from an EU supplier, VAT is **not charged** on the invoice; instead, your SL self-assesses it on the Modelo 303 in the same quarter and deducts it immediately (net zero if used for business).
- **Source**: [Agencia Tributaria — Tipos impositivos IVA](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/calculo-iva-repercutido-clientes/tipos-impositivos-iva.html)

### Administrator compensation — net-of-tax comparison (nómina vs. dividends)

**No single best path; depends on company profitability, administrator ownership, and marginal rates. Outline:**

| Channel | Deductible in IS? | IRPF tax on admin | Social security | Timing/flexibility | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Salary (nómina)** | YES (if documented, real services) | Yes (19–45% progressive) | YES, RETA mandatory (€448/mo base min.) | Monthly/predictable | Stable income; demonstrates legitimacy |
| **Dividends** | NO (never deductible) | YES (19–30% via base del ahorro IRPF); no withholding if over €600/year | No additional social security | After profit earned; flexible timing | Bonus/profit-sharing post-tax |
| **Invoice for services** (if not salary) | YES (if bona fide) | YES (treated as self-employed income) | Depends on classification; risky | Ad-hoc; must show real work | Specialized contractor role |

- **Common strategy**: 60% salary + 40% dividends (rough example), chosen to balance IS deduction + IRPF tax + RETA burden.
- **Hacienda scrutiny (Plan de Control Tributario 2026)**: Administrator compensation is a priority audit target; ensure real services, documentation, and reasonableness.
- **Source**: [Tedeclaro — Retribución administrador único 2026](https://tedeclaro.com/retribucion-administrador-unico/); [STS 546/2025](https://copilotgestoria.com/blog/retribucion-administrador-sl-2026-nomina-factura-dividendo-hacienda-guia-gestorias)
