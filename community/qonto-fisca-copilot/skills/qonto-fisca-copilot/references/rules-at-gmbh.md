## 🔔 Transaction-triggered nudges

### Reverse-charge (autoliquidation) VAT on foreign B2B services

**Rule:** When an Austrian GmbH receives services from a foreign enterprise with no Austrian premises, the GmbH **self-accounts for VAT**—the foreign supplier does NOT charge VAT, and the GmbH declares it as reverse charge in the VAT return.

- ✅ **Invoice requirement:** Foreign supplier must issue invoice with no VAT stated; must note "tax liability transferred to recipient" + both VAT ID numbers (UID). [USP.gv.at](https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/umsatzsteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-zur-umsatzsteuer/umsaetze-mit-auslandsbezug/reverse-charge.html)
- ✅ **Local mechanism:** Declare on monthly/quarterly VAT return (Umsatzsteuererklärung) in the reverse-charge line; input VAT recoverable if standard-rated supplies. [USP.gv.at](https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/umsatzsteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-zur-umsatzsteuer/umsaetze-mit-auslandsbezug/reverse-charge.html)
- ⚠️ **Exceptions (no reverse charge):** Real estate, passenger transport, catering, events/conferences, highway tolls, mobile phone devices. (Sourced from secondary sources; cross-check against latest BMF guidance for edge cases)
- **Sources:** [USP.gv.at — Reverse Charge](https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/umsatzsteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-zur-umsatzsteuer/umsaetze-mit-auslandsbezug/reverse-charge.html)

### Invoice & documentation for VAT/tax deduction

**Rule:** All deductible expenses require documented invoices; Austrian GmbH must retain invoices ≥7 years for audit.

- ✗→ **B2G e-invoicing:** Mandatory since **1 January 2014** (NOT 18 April 2020) for government suppliers. Format: ebInterface XML or Peppol BIS 3.0. Submit via USP/eRechnung.gv.at. [USP.gv.at — e-invoicing](https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/umsatzsteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-zur-umsatzsteuer/vorsteuerabzug-und-rechnung/e-rechnung-an-die-oeffentliche-verwaltung.html)
- ✅ **B2B e-invoicing:** Voluntary (no mandate yet). [USP.gv.at](https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/umsatzsteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-zur-umsatzsteuer/vorsteuerabzug-und-rechnung/e-rechnung.html)
- ⚠️ **ViDA directive:** Adopted May 2026 (confirmed). B2B e-reporting timeline: Article 2 from 1 Jan 2027, Article 3 from 1 Jan 2028; mandatory B2B e-invoicing deferred to **1 July 2030** (not 1 Jan 2028). Austria's detailed implementation roadmap for Peppol/B2B not yet finalized as of July 2026.
- ✅ **Paper invoices:** Accepted for B2B if parties agree; must still meet content rules (VAT ID, net amount, tax rate, due date). [WKO](https://www.wko.at/en/requirements-for-an-invoice)
- ✅ **Retention:** 7 years for authenticity, integrity, and legibility. [USP.gv.at](https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/umsatzsteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-zur-umsatzsteuer/vorsteuerabzug-und-rechnung/e-rechnung.html)

### Mileage allowance (Fahrtkosten) — real deduction

**Rule:** GmbH can deduct business travel costs at flat rate + document expenses above that.

- ✅ **Flat rate:** €0.50/km for car, motorcycle, bicycle, e-bike (standard deduction; includes fuel, tolls, parking, depreciation) as of July 1, 2025. [BMF](https://www.bmf.gv.at/themen/steuern/kraftfahrzeuge/kilometergeld.html)
- ✅ **Annual cap:** 30,000 km/year at flat rate (€15,000 max); excess km must be documented by actual receipt. [USP.gv.at](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuerliche-gewinnermittlung/weitere-informationen-zur-steuerlichen-gewinnermittlung/betriebseinnahmen-und-ausgaben/reisekosten.html)
- ⚠️ **Passengers:** +€0.15/km per additional passenger (names must be documented). (Not confirmed in official sources; secondary source standard.)
- ✅ **Luxury vehicles:** Capped at acquisition cost €40,000 for tax depreciation purposes. [USP.gv.at](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuerliche-gewinnermittlung/weitere-informationen-zur-steuerlichen-gewinnermittlung/betriebseinnahmen-und-ausgaben/gesetzliche-afa-saetze.html)

### Business meals & equipment — real expenses (NOT forfait)

**Rule:** GmbH deducts actual business meal costs and equipment; **NOT** a micro-enterprise flat-rate regime.

- ⚠️ **Business meals:** Fully deductible if related to business activity (meals with clients, staff events, conferences). No hard cap (unlike Germany's 70% rule). Employer-funded staff meals/events up to €365/employee/year are tax-free for employees (not a limit on GmbH deduction, but a benefit cap). (Secondary sources; not confirmed in official BMF/USP guidance.)
- ✅ **Equipment (Betriebsanlage):** Full cost deductible via depreciation (see depreciation table below) or immediate write-off if <€1,000 net value (effective since 1 Jan 2023). [USP.gv.at](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuerliche-gewinnermittlung/weitere-informationen-zur-steuerlichen-gewinnermittlung/betriebseinnahmen-und-ausgaben/geringwertige-wirtschaftsgueter.html)

## 🎯 Reliefs / niches to activate (2026)

### VAT small-business exemption (Kleinunternehmerregelung)

**Rule:** If annual turnover ≤ €55,000, GmbH exempt from VAT registration and returns.

- ✅ **Threshold:** €55,000 net (changed 1 Jan 2025; previously €35,000). Calculated on **entire agreed remuneration** (all revenue, no deductions). [USP.gv.at](https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/umsatzsteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-zur-umsatzsteuer/weitere-steuertatbestaende-und-befreiungen/kleinunternehmen.html)
- ✅ **Automatic qualification:** No application needed; if you qualify, you are exempt by law.
- ⚠️ **Opting out:** Can waive exemption via Form U12 (binding 5 years) to charge/reclaim VAT (useful if most customers are B2B VAT-registered and can recover). (Not confirmed in official sources; secondary guidance.)
- ⚠️ **One-time relief:** If you exceed threshold in a year, you **lose exemption for that year and all subsequent years**—cannot re-use exemption within rolling 5-year period. (Tolerance rule introduced 2025 may affect this; confirm locally.)
- ✅ **Note:** Reverse-charge VAT on foreign B2B services **still applies** even under small-business exemption (universal EU rule); must declare reverse-charge transactions on VAT return.
- **Sources:** [USP.gv.at — Kleinunternehmen](https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/umsatzsteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-zur-umsatzsteuer/weitere-steuertatbestaende-und-befreiungen/kleinunternehmen.html)

### Investment allowance (Investitionsfreibetrag) 2025–2026

**Rule:** GmbH can claim accelerated deduction on new equipment & machinery.

- ✅ **Standard rate:** 10% of acquisition cost (deductible in addition to depreciation). [USP.gv.at](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuerliche-gewinnermittlung/weitere-informationen-zur-steuerlichen-gewinnermittlung/betriebseinnahmen-und-ausgaben/investitionsfreibetrag.html)
- ✅ **Temporary rate (Nov 2025 – Dec 2026):** **20%** (doubled for 2-year window). [WKO](https://www.wko.at/steuern/investitionsfreibetrag)
- ✅ **Ecological investments (Öko-IFB):** 22% (Nov 2025 – Dec 2026; normal: 15%). [WKO](https://www.wko.at/steuern/investitionsfreibetrag)
- ✅ **Cap:** €1 million per year of eligible acquisitions. [USP.gv.at](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuerliche-gewinnermittlung/weitere-informationen-zur-steuerlichen-gewinnermittlung/betriebseinnahmen-und-ausgaben/investitionsfreibetrag.html)

### FlexKapG startup incentives (introduced 1 Jan 2024)

**Rule:** New flexible capital company (FlexKapG) with reduced capital & compliance burden; same tax treatment as GmbH.

- ✅ **Minimum capital:** €10,000 minimum (vs. GmbH minimum €10,000). Enables multiple share classes for funding rounds. [WKO](https://www.wko.at/gruendung/flexible-kapitalgesellschaft-flexkapg-)
- ✅ **Minimum tax:** €500/year (same as GmbH as of 2024). [USP.gv.at](https://startup.usp.gv.at/en/gruendung/gruendungsfahrplan/gruendungsfahrplan-gmbh-flexkapg)
- ✅ **Tax rate:** 23% corporate income tax (identical to GmbH). [WKO](https://www.wko.at/wirtschaftsrecht/flexible-kapitalgesellschaft)
- ⚠️ **Employee share schemes:** Tax-free grant of shares to employees (taxed only on sale, not receipt); reduced social contributions under conditions. (Described in regulatory intent; not confirmed in detailed official guidance.)
- ✅ **Share transfer:** Can be documented by notary or lawyer (simpler than GmbH handover rules). [WKO](https://www.wko.at/gruendung/flexible-kapitalgesellschaft-flexkapg-)
- **Sources:** [WKO — FlexKapG](https://www.wko.at/gruendung/flexible-kapitalgesellschaft-flexkapg-); [USP.gv.at](https://startup.usp.gv.at/gruendung/ueberlegungen-im-vorfeld/gmbh-flexkapg)
