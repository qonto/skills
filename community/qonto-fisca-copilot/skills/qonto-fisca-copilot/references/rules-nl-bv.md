## 🔔 Transaction-triggered nudges

### **Reverse-charge VAT (verleggingsregeling) on B2B foreign services**
- **✅ Mechanism**: When purchasing services from a foreign entrepreneur (EU or non-EU), VAT is reverse-charged: you (the buyer) report it as both output VAT and input VAT, net effect typically zero. — [Belastingdienst reverse-charging VAT guide](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontenten/belastingdienst/business/vat/vat_in_the_netherlands/calculating_vat/reverse-charging_vat)
- **⚠️ Invoice requirement**: Foreign supplier must NOT charge VAT; wording requirement not confirmed in official sources (practice often uses "VAT reverse-charged").
- **✅ Declaration**: Report in Box 4a (output VAT) and Box 5b (input VAT) of your VAT return.
- **✅ Applies universally** regardless of your VAT exemption status (KOR); all Dutch businesses must apply this.

### **Invoice required to deduct and justify expenses**
- **✅ Retention period**: 7 years (10 years for real estate–related records). — [Business.gov.nl invoice requirements](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/invoice-requirements/)
- **✅ Invoice content**: Must show supplier name, VAT number (if applicable), description, amount, date.
- **✅ Digital copies accepted**: Photographs of receipts and digital storage are permitted if complete and readable.
- **⚠️ B2G e-invoicing**: Mandatory for Dutch central government contracts (Peppol or Digipoort networks). Exact implementation date (January 2019) not independently confirmed in current official sources.
- **⚠️ B2B e-invoicing (voluntary as of 2026)**: No domestic B2B mandate yet; expected from 1 January 2030 under ViDA Directive (timeline not independently confirmed).
- **✅ Audit risk**: Any deductible cost without proof will be rejected; you pay tax + potential fine.

### **Mileage deduction (private vehicle for business)**
- **✅ Standard deduction**: €0.25 per kilometre (2026 rate, increased from €0.23). — [Belastingdienst official rate](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/zakelijk-gebruik-privevervoermiddel-2026)
- **✅ Applies to self-employed and employed**: Both may deduct €0.25/km for business use in 2026; employed individuals can claim employer reimbursement at the same rate. — [Business.gov.nl company car use guide](https://business.gov.nl/finance-and-taxes/deductibles-and-schemes/company-use-of-a-private-car/)
- **✅ All modes**: Public transport, taxi, and airplane at 100% with receipt proof.
- **✅ No double-dipping**: Cannot claim additional fuel/maintenance costs; they are embedded in the allowance.

### **Business meals & representation expenses**
- **✅ Deduction rate**: 80% of the cost (not 100%).
- **✅ Threshold method alternative**: First €5,700/year is non-deductible; amounts above are deductible at 100%.
- **✅ Choice of method**: Use either the threshold or 80% rule in your return; you pick the better option.
- **✅ VAT on meals NOT deductible**: You cannot recover VAT on business meals (you are treated as a consumer).
- **✅ Scope**: Includes representation, conferences, seminars, and study trips. — [Business.gov.nl deductibles guide](https://business.gov.nl/finance-and-taxes/deductibles-and-schemes/deducting-business-expenses/)

### **DGA salary must meet "customary loon" rule**
- **✅ Minimum threshold (2026)**: €58,000/year (increased from €56,000 in 2025). — [Belastingdienst official DGA guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/loon_en_aanmerkelijk_belang)
- **✅ Three-part test**: DGA salary must be **the highest of**:
  1. Salary of the most comparable employee outside the BV;
  2. Salary of the highest-paid employee within your BV or affiliated company;
  3. €58,000 statutory minimum.
- **⚠️ Tax Authority scrutiny**: Belastingdienst will tighten controls on under-declared DGA salaries in 2026–2027. (Not independently verified in official source)
- **✅ No salary cap**: There is no maximum; above-market salaries are permitted (and may reduce box 2 dividend exposure).
- **✅ Withholding**: You must withhold payroll tax (loonheffingen) on the DGA's salary and file wages separately.

### **Equipment & asset capitalization**
- **✅ Under €450**: Fully deductible in the year of purchase. — [Business.gov.nl deductibles guide](https://business.gov.nl/finance-and-taxes/deductibles-and-schemes/deducting-business-expenses/)
- **✅ €450+**: Must be depreciated over useful lifespan (formula: (purchase price − residual value) ÷ years).
- **✅ Business-specific items**: Work clothing with logos, briefcases, tools are 100% deductible.
- **⚠️ Home workspace**: Generally 0% deductible (exceptions apply; check with tax advisor).

## 🎯 Reliefs & niches

### **Small Business VAT Exemption (KOR — Kleineondernemersregeling)**
- **✅ Turnover ceiling**: €20,000/year (both current and prior calendar year must stay below). — [Business.gov.nl KOR scheme](https://business.gov.nl/subsidies-and-schemes/small-businesses-scheme/)
- **✅ EU extension**: From January 2025, total turnover across all 27 EU member states must remain below €100,000.
- **✅ Benefit**: No VAT filing required; no VAT charge on invoices; no input VAT recovery (simplified accounting).
- **✅ When exemption ends**: Immediately upon exceeding €20,000 in a calendar year; you must then register for VAT and start charging 21%.
- **✅ Check eligibility annually**: Threshold applies both backward (prior year) and forward (current/next year).

### **DGA Excessive Borrowing (€500,000 threshold)**
- **⚠️ to verify**: DGAs borrowing more than €500,000 from their BV may face tax consequences (borrowing above this threshold is subject to Box 2 taxation). Specific relief mechanisms and interest deduction provisions require confirmation with a tax advisor. — [KVK 2026 thresholds](https://www.kvk.nl/en/finance/dutch-tax-rates-in-2026/)

### **Dividend withholding tax credit (15%)**
- **✅ Withholding rate**: When BV pays a dividend to the DGA, the company withholds 15% dividend tax (dividendbelasting). — [Belastingdienst dividend tax](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontenten/belastingdienst/business/dividend-tax/dividend-tax)
- **✅ Credit mechanism**: This 15% is credited against the DGA's final Box 2 tax liability when they file their personal return.
- **✅ Net effect**: The DGA ultimately pays the higher of 15% withholding or final Box 2 rate (24.5%–31%), depending on total box 2 income.
