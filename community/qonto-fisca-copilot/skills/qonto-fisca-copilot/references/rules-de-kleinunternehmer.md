## 🔔 Transaction-triggered nudges

### Reverse-Charge (§13b UStG) applies EVEN under Kleinunternehmerregelung
- **Scenario:** Invoice received from foreign (non-German) B2B service provider (e.g., AWS, Google Ads Ireland, OpenAI, Figma, Adobe).
- **Your obligation:** You assume VAT liability. You self-calculate VAT on the net invoice amount and report it on your tax return (Umsatzsteuererklärung).
- **Critical catch:** Unlike standard VAT-registered businesses, Kleinunternehmer **cannot recover the self-calculated VAT as input tax**. You bear the full cost.
- **Action required:** Obtain a VAT ID (USt-IdNr.) to manage reverse-charge correctly and file necessary EU sales listings (Zusammenfassende Meldung) when selling to EU businesses.
- **Reporting:** File quarterly Zusammenfassende Meldung (ZM) if you have any EU reverse-charge transactions.
- **Source:** §13b UStG (effective through 2026); [Norman Finance Reverse-Charge Guide](https://norman.finance/de/blog/reverse-charge)

### Invoice requirement: Must state VAT exemption status
- Every invoice to German customers and EU private persons must carry: ✅ **"Gemäß §19 Abs. 1 UStG wird keine Umsatzsteuer berechnet"** (no VAT is charged under §19 UStG).
- No separate VAT line is shown; price is net.
- **Source:** [§14b UStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__14b.html)

### E-invoicing mandate (ZUGFeRD 2.1 / XRechnung)
- **Receiving (effective ✅ 1 January 2025):** All businesses—including Kleinunternehmer—must be capable of receiving structured e-invoices in ZUGFeRD or XRechnung format. Simply providing an email address is sufficient; no dedicated system required.
- **Sending (effective ✅ 1 January 2027):** As of 1 January 2027, Germany's B2B e-invoicing mandate applies to businesses above €800,000 turnover. Kleinunternehmer with §19 invoices (no VAT) are in a grey area—most tax advisors recommend implementing ZUGFeRD to future-proof workflows.
- **Preferred formats:** ZUGFeRD 2.1 (PDF + embedded XML, EN 16931–compliant) or Germany's XRechnung standard.
- **Source:** [BMF E-Rechnung FAQ](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/FAQ/e-rechnung.html); [BMF E-Rechnung Einführung 2025](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Umsatzsteuer/Umsatzsteuer-Anwendungserlass/2025-10-15-einfuehrung-obligatorische-e-rechnung.html)

### No VAT filing requirement for pure Kleinunternehmer
- Since tax year 2024: ✅ no monthly/quarterly Umsatzsteuervoranmeldung (VAT pre-returns) required.
- No annual Umsatzsteuererklärung unless the Finanzamt explicitly requests one.
- **Exception:** If you issue reverse-charge invoices to EU businesses, you must file quarterly Zusammenfassende Meldung.
- **Source:** [BMF Kleinunternehmerregelung](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Umsatzsteuer/Umsatzsteuer-Anwendungserlass/2025-11-10-kleinunternehmerregelung.pdf); [Accountable Guide](https://www.accountable.de/en/help-center/kleinunternehmer-in-2025-everything-you-need-to-know/)

## 🎯 Reliefs & niches to activate

### Gewerbesteuer exemption for Freiberufler (NOT Einzelunternehmen)
- **Freiberufler** (liberal professions: doctors, lawyers, architects, engineers, consultants, artists, journalists, tax advisors) are **exempt from Gewerbesteuer**.
- **Einzelunternehmen** (commercial sole traders) must pay Gewerbesteuer (see 📅 Thresholds).
- **Relief:** Freiberufler avoid 8–17% effective trade tax. Major advantage if turnover grows beyond Kleinunternehmer limits.
- **Source:** [CountryTaxCalc Freelancer Tax Guide 2026](https://www.countrytaxcalc.com/tax-guides/germany/freelancer-steuerklasse-guide-2026/)

### Deductible Betriebsausgaben (business expenses)
All ordinary and necessary business expenses reduce taxable profit—unlimited in amount, provided documented and reasonable:

- **Home office:** ✅ €6/day flat-rate (max €1,260/year, 210 days) **OR** actual costs (rent share, utilities, internet, renovations) if dedicated room ≥90% business use.
- **Equipment & software:** Computers and software fully deductible in year of purchase (regardless of cost) under 2021 BMF ruling; or depreciation over useful life if shared-use.
- **Travel & meals:** Business travel (train, hotel, flights), accommodation, ✅ meal allowances (€14–28/day for business trips) fully deductible. Commuting (home ↔ first workplace): ✅ €0.38/km Entfernungspauschale from the first km as of 1 Jan 2026 (confirmed BMF).
- **Professional subscriptions, books, training:** Unlimited deduction if business-related.
- **Client meals & entertainment:** ✅ 70% deductible if receipts show date, place, purpose, attendees. (Note: 25% rule does NOT apply to Freiberufler meal entertainment)
- **Professional insurance, accountant fees, chamber membership:** Fully deductible.
- **Vehicle expenses (business trip, own car):** ✗→ **€0.30/km** (Reisekosten-Pauschale, BMF) **or** actual cost (fuel, maintenance, insurance, depreciation) — not both. ⚠️ Do **not** confuse this with the €0.38/km *commuting* Entfernungspauschale above — the business-trip own-car rate is €0.30/km.
- **Home internet:** Prorated share (e.g., 50% home office use = 50% deductible).

**Documentation:** Keep all receipts and invoices for ≥10 years (§14b UStG). ✅

- **Source:** [§4 Abs. 5 EStG Homeoffice](https://ao.bundesfinanzministerium.de/lsth/2026/B-Anhaenge/Anhang-19/I/inhalt.html); [Entfernungspauschale 2026](https://ao.bundesfinanzministerium.de/lsth/2026/B-Anhaenge/Anhang-14/inhalt.html); [BMF Reisekosten 2026](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Lohnsteuer/2025-12-05-steuerliche-behandlung-reisekosten-2026.pdf)

### Simplified profit calculation (EÜR) for most sole traders
- Freiberufler and Einzelunternehmen with turnover ≤€600,000 ⚠️ to verify (2026 threshold raised from €420,000 in 2025) **may** use simplified Einnahmen-Überschuss-Rechnung (EÜR) instead of full bookkeeping.
- EÜR: Revenue – Expenses = Profit; no balance sheet required.
- If turnover exceeds €600,000, double-entry bookkeeping and annual accounts (Bilanzierung) become mandatory.

### Five-year binding for §19 election
- Once you claim Kleinunternehmerregelung, you are bound for a ✅ **minimum of 5 tax years**. Voluntary opt-out to standard VAT taxation is binding for the same period.
- Early termination only allowed if circumstances (e.g., significant business expansion) justify it.
- **Source:** [§19 Abs. 3 UStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__19.html)
