## 🔔 Transaction-triggered nudges

**Reverse-charge / autoliquidation VAT on foreign B2B services**
- When **non-established supplier** invoices your SRL/BV for services (outside immovable property, passenger transport, catering, vehicle leasing, conference/exhibition access), **you (the customer) self-assess VAT** ("reverse charge," art. 51 § 2, 5° VAT Code).
  - Invoice text: "Autoliquidation – Art 51 § 2, 5°"
  - Applies **even if you claim VAT exemption** as a small business (reverse charge overrides exemption).
  - You deduct the VAT you reverse-charge on your VAT return.
- **Local declaration:** Reverse-charged services appear on your periodic VAT return; no separate reversal needed.
- [Marosa reverse-charge guide](https://marosavat.com/manual/vat/belgium/reverse-charge/)

**Invoice required to deduct/justify**
- Retain **all invoices** for supplies and expenses; Belgian tax audit scrutinizes missing or undated documents.
- **E-invoicing mandatory for B2B** ✅ from **1 January 2026** ✅ (Peppol BIS 3.0 format ✅; 3-month tolerance through 31 March 2026 ✅ with no penalties if reasonable effort shown ✅).
  - Paper invoices between your company and other registered VAT businesses are no longer acceptable ✅.
  - B2C invoices (invoices to consumers) are exempt from e-invoicing requirement ✅.
- [Belgium e-invoicing guide](https://marosa.com/vat-news/e-invoicing-b2b-belgium-complete-guide-january-2026) ✅

**Mileage / business meals / equipment — real-expense regime**
- **Mileage allowance:** €0.4449/km (July 2025–June 2026 rate) ✅, tax-deductible as professional expense ✅ (no taxable benefit to director).
  - Document: trip purpose, km driven, date ✅.
  - Rate is indexed and updated annually ✅; verify current rate before filing ✅.
- **Business meals:** Only **69% of restaurant/catering costs** deductible ✅; remainder is personal ✅.
  - Per diem (fixed daily meal allowance) on business trips: deductible if documented ✅ (trip duration > 5 hours outside office) ✅.
- **Company vehicles:** Only **zero-emission vehicles** purchased before 1 January 2027 are 100% deductible ✅ for ISoc purposes. From 2027, zero-emission cars face gradual reduction to 67.5% by 2031 ✅; non-zero-emission cars phased out ✅.
  - Document: vehicle registration, business-use evidence, fuel/maintenance records.
- SRL/BV are **not** small-trader / forfait regimes — these deductions apply to real expenses, not flat-rate allowances.
- [Deductible expenses guide (mileage, per diem)](https://www.deductibles.be/) ✅, [Rydoo compliance](https://www.rydoo.com/compliance/belgium/belgian-mileage/) ✅

## 🎯 Reliefs / niches to activate

**VVPRbis dividend withholding tax — critical timing** ✅
- **Until 30 June 2026:** Dividends distributed from profits of prior years (or declared at general meeting before 1 July 2026) are subject to **15% withholding tax** ✅ (VVPR bis regime).
  - Conditions: shares held since ≥ 1 July 2013 ✅ (original cash contribution); SRL/BV as small company (turnover < €11.25M ✅, balance sheet < €6M ✅).
  - **Action:** If planning interim/final dividends, ensure allocation date (board/general-meeting decision) is **before 1 July 2026** ✅ to lock in 15%.
- **From 1 July 2026 onwards:** Withholding tax on VVPRbis dividends rises to **18%** ✅.
  - For contributions made from 1 January 2026, the intermediate 20% rate is abolished; 15% applies for distributions after year 3. ⚠️ to verify (technical scope unclear)
- **Liquidation reserves:** Distributions after 3-year holding period: 6.5% → 9.8% (from 1 July 2026) ✅. New reserves (created after 31 Dec 2025) face 30% rate if distributed within 3 years. ✅
- [VVPRbis dividend tax changes](https://www.vandelanotte.be/en/news/higher-withholding-tax-on-vvprbis-dividends-and-liquidation-reserves-from-1-july-2026) ✅

**Reduced ISoc rate (20% on first €100k profit) — director remuneration lever** ✅
- To qualify, **one director must earn minimum €50,000/year gross remuneration** ✅ (indexed annually on CPI ✅).
  - Benefits in kind may not exceed 20% of this remuneration ✅ (max €10k of €50k).
  - If taxable profit < €50k, remuneration floor can be lowered to taxable profit (calculated as operating profit ÷ 2).
  - Does **not** apply during first four fiscal years after SRL/BV incorporation ✅.
- Once threshold is met, **€0–€100k taxable profit taxed at 20%** ✅, profit above €100k at 25% ✅.
- [2026 director remuneration rules](https://www.bnpparibasfortis.be/en/public/article/remuneration-dirigeant-arizona) ✅, [Accountable reduced-rate conditions](https://www.accountable.eu/en-be/blog/reduced-corporate-tax-rate/) ✅

**Mileage allowance as deductible expense** (already noted above, but reinforced as relief)
- Claim **quarterly or annual deduction** on corporate tax form 275; align documentation with VAT records.
