# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-project-burn

Invoke with `/qonto-project-burn <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started

- "What did each of my projects actually cost?"
- "Are we over budget on project Alpha?"

## Going further

- "Which projects are past 80% of their declared budget?"
- "At the current run rate, when does project Alpha cross its budget?"
- "What did project Alpha cost this month, and who are its top suppliers?"
- "Show me the unattributed transactions — the spend that belongs to no project yet."
- "Here are our budgets: Alpha €20k, Beta €8k — map my labels to these projects and remember it."
- "Which project burns fastest right now?"

## Chain it

- "Post every project's burn status to Linear — show me the comment before it goes out."
- "Now compute the full per-project P&L, revenue included, with qonto-project-pnl."
- "Some of that recurring project spend looks dormant — audit it with qonto-subscription-audit."

> ⚠️ Nothing is posted to your team tracker without a preview and your explicit go — the skill shows the exact comment text first.
