# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-rebill

Invoke with `/qonto-rebill <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started

- "Which expenses did I advance for my clients this quarter?"
- "Rebill my project expenses to the right clients."

## Going further

- "What did I advance for client Acme since January — travel, hosting, everything?"
- "Did I forget to rebill anything from past quarters?"
- "Explain the difference between a disbursement and a rebilled expense before I choose."
- "Prepare the rebill for this client with a 10% handling fee on top."
- "Group my client-attributable expenses by project and show which lines still miss a receipt."
- "Prepare the invoice for Acme — draft only, I'll review before anything goes out."

## Chain it

- "Fill the missing receipts first with qonto-receipt-hunter, then rebuild the rebill table."
- "The invoice went out — chase it if it's still unpaid next month with qonto-invoice-chaser."
- "Do the same exercise for last quarter and compare the recovered totals."

> ⚠️ Invoices are always created as drafts and sent only after your explicit confirmation — nothing leaves your account name without your go.
