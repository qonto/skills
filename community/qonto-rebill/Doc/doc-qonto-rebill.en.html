qonto-rebill

The expenses you advanced for clients, literally recovered — a line-by-line rebill invoice, receipts inventoried.

💶 Money literally recovered🔒 Single write = a draft ⚖️ Disbursement vs rebill explained📄 PR-ready

🎯 Why this matters

Freelancers and agencies advance money for their clients — plugins, stock photos, hosting, travel, mission meals — and forget to rebill half of it. The skill goes and gets that money back:

1

Finds client expenses

Client-named Qonto labels, teams, or history-assisted attribution — one transaction at a time, never silently.

2

Groups + receipts

Expenses grouped per client and period, with the receipts already attached inventoried — gaps flagged per line.

3

The VAT choice, explained

Disbursement (outside VAT) vs rebilled expense (VAT at the main service's rate): the skill compares and asks — it never decides alone.

4

Draft invoice

One line per expense, optional 5-10% handling fee — sent only after explicit confirmation.

Would someone use this on a Monday morning? It's the end-of-quarter ritual that pays for itself the first time it runs.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • No client labels? → interactive attribution: the skill shortlists candidates (counterparties previously attributed, mission-typed spend: SaaS, stock photos, hosting, transport, meals) and asks one transaction at a time — then offers to label going forward so next quarter is automatic
  • Labels matched to invoicing clients: case/accent normalization — the label "acme studio" = the client "ACME Studio"
  • Card expenses straddling two periods: grouped by emitted_at, not settled_at (settlement lands 1-2 days later)
  • Receipt missing on a line → flagged, with a pointer to qonto-receipt-hunter before sending
  • Empty account, expense-free period, client missing from invoicing, non-French company: the skill says what it can and cannot do
  • Pagination ≤ 50 everywhere · get_organization always first (bank_account_id required by list_transactions)

⚖️ Disbursement vs rebilled expense (the table the skill shows)

CriterionDisbursement (débours, art. 267 II-2° CGI)Rebilled expense (standard)
PrinciplePaid in the client's name and on their behalf (mandate)Paid in your own name, recharged as part of your price
MarginForbidden — exact amount, euro for euroAllowed (handling fee 5-10%…)
VATOutside VAT scope: no VAT on the line, input VAT not deductibleVAT at the main service's rate (often 20%) — even if the underlying cost was 10% or VAT-free
ReceiptOriginal invoice in the client's name, handed overOriginal invoice in your name, kept in your books
BooksThird-party account (not revenue)Revenue
CountryFrench regimeUniversal mechanics; outside France → local accountant

The skill explains and asks — it never picks a regime on its own (per line when regimes are mixed), and every report recommends accountant validation.

🛡 Guardrails

  • MCP-created invoices are real: draft until explicit confirmation, never presented as sent, rehearsals on a fictitious client + delete_client_invoice
  • No silent attribution, ever: every uncertain expense is asked; nothing invented — no amount, no receipt, no client
  • The disbursement/rebill choice belongs to the user (with their accountant) — the skill explains and asks
  • Honest degradation (no labels, empty account, non-French country) · IBANs masked (last 4 digits) · pagination ≤ 50
