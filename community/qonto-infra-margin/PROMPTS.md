# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-infra-margin

Invoke with `/qonto-infra-margin <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started

- "What does my infra actually cost, and per user?"
- "How much am I spending on AI APIs, and how is it trending?"

## Going further

- "Isolate my infrastructure spend over the last 12 months, vendor by vendor."
- "Which side-projects are costing me money with zero traffic?"
- "Is my gross margin drifting? Show infra as a share of receipts, month by month."
- "Compare my hosting bill this quarter against the same quarter last year."
- "Run the analysis without product telemetry — just the infra P&L from bank debits."
- "Build me an HTML dashboard of the infra unit economics."

## Chain it

- "Now check whether any of those infra tools are zombie subscriptions — qonto-subscription-audit."
- "Watch for future price hikes on these vendors with qonto-subscription-guardian."
- "Fold this infra picture into the monthly numbers with qonto-monthly-close."
