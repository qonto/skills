qonto-grant-scout

Your bank account knows what you invest in — public databases know who gets helped for it. This skill introduces them.

🔎 Read-only — zero writes🧭 Real profile from the flows 🗂 Dated sources (data.gouv.fr)🤝 Optional multi-MCP

🎯 Why this matters

France runs hundreds of public aid schemes for small businesses — and most SMEs claim none of them, because nobody has time to dig. Yet the account already tells the story of what the company invests in. The skill makes the introduction:

1

Real company profile

Sector (NAF when present, inferred from the flows otherwise), region, size, age — all from the account, zero forms.

2

Real spending structure

12–24 months of flows classified: training, equipment, digital, hiring, export, energy — each category priced in €/year.

3

Datagouv crossing

Search across the public aid referentials — each dataset's update date is checked and displayed.

4

Honest shortlist

Every lead: criteria to confirm, dated source, one concrete next step. Never "you are eligible" — always "worth checking".

Would someone use this on a Monday morning? "Am I leaving public money on the table?" is a question every founder asks — and never has time to answer.

⚙️ How it works

Structural honesty — what keeps the skill credible on uncertain data

  • Every lead carries its source AND its date: aid referentials on data.gouv.fr are heterogeneous and sometimes stale — get_dataset_info checks freshness; a dataset older than ~18 months degrades the lead's tag
  • Never "you are eligible": schemes open, close, and change criteria — every lead is "worth checking", with the criteria the dataset exposes and the body to contact
  • Non-queryable resource (PDF, malformed CSV): the lead becomes a sourced link to the dataset page, stated as such
  • Datagouv MCP absent or failing: spending profile + generic aid families + the right official portals — stated plainly, and the skill continues
  • Sector missing from get_organization: inferred from counterparties, confidence lowered, user can correct
  • Labels unavailable? list_cash_flow_categories returns 403 on the claude.ai connector → fallback to list_labels · card spending matched by emitted_at · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere

🛡 Guardrails

  • Zero writes; only generic keywords reach Datagouv — never amounts, names, or IBANs
  • Never "you are eligible" — every lead is dated, sourced, phrased "to verify"; confirmation with the issuing body or the accountant recommended
  • Honest degradation: no Datagouv MCP, empty account, undetectable sector, non-FR country → the skill states its limits and never invents a scheme, an amount, or a deadline
  • IBANs masked (last 4 digits) · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere
