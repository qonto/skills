🎯 Why this matters

The number-one fear of French company directors is the tax audit — and nobody knows where they're vulnerable until the letter arrives. The skill flips the roles: it screens Qonto transactions against the 8 verification axes real auditors use, and hands over the report first:

1

The 8-axis screen

Lavish expenses, personal spending, director's current account, VAT vs receipts, benefits in kind, duplicates, cash, round amounts.

2

Sourced findings

Every point = a precise transaction + the tax rule cited + a risk level + a fix. Never an accusation.

3

Priced exposure

Reclaimable VAT, corporate-tax reintegrations, interest and penalties — educational orders of magnitude.

4

Remediation plan

The fixes to make BEFORE the tax office asks — then re-run the skill and watch the grade improve.

Would someone run this on a Monday morning? It's the quarterly "sleep well" ritual — and priceless the week before the accountant's annual review.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • No labels, no categories? → the skill screens raw counterparties and merchant names; a user's own "perso" label, when present, is surfaced gently
  • No attachments anywhere? → axis 4 becomes the headline finding with the full VAT amount at stake — that IS the value, not a failure mode
  • Detection lives in native fields: attachment_ids, attachment_required, vat_amount do most of the work; list_transaction_attachments only verifies transactions about to be flagged
  • Card patterns read on emitted_at (settled_at lags 1–2 days — a Sunday purchase can show up on Tuesday)
  • 403 missing oauth scope on list_cash_flow_categories → expected on the claude.ai connector, labels as fallback
  • Empty or young account, non-French company: the skill says what it can and can't verify — and which axes it skipped
  • False positives are owned up front: a flagged restaurant can be 100 % legitimate — the report says so in as many words

🛡 Guardrails

  • Fully read-only: no write tool is ever called — a commitment, not a setting
  • Never an accusation: "point of attention", not "fraud"; a flagged expense may be perfectly legitimate — the skill may under- or over-estimate for lack of context, and says so
  • Not tax advice, not the administration's position: educational simulation, accountant validation recalled in every report
  • Non-French org → universal axes only, French articles never cited outside their scope
  • IBANs masked, sensitive names masked in any export · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere
