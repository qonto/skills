🧾 Form 3310-CA3, box by box🔒 Zero writes — read-only 🇫🇷 Cash-basis VAT handled📄 PR-ready

🎯 Why this matters

Every month, the same chore: reconstruct collected and deductible VAT, then copy the right amounts into the right boxes on the tax portal. The skill does the preparation work from the Qonto account — a preparation aid, NOT a filing:

1

Collected VAT per rate

Bases and tax at 20 / 10 / 5.5 / 2.1% → boxes 08, 09, 9B, 11 — on debits or on receipts, per the detected chargeability.

2

Deductible as a floor

Summed vat_amount + supplier invoices, fixed assets (box 19) split out when detectable — floor announced with the count of untagged debits.

3

Net position

Line 28 payable or credit to carry forward (25/27) — cents plus the form's whole-euro rounding.

4

Consistency checks

Vs previous months, vs past DGFIP payments, vs statements — every discrepancy flagged ⚠️ before you copy.

Would someone use this on filing morning? It replaces the spreadsheet ritual entirely.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • Chargeability detected, never asked: vat_payment_condition: "on_receipts" on client invoices → collected VAT follows the month's received payments, matched to invoices (amount + counterparty + reference) — the case most tools miss; mixed portfolios handled per invoice
  • Filing frequency from DGFIP history: monthly debits → monthly CA3; quarterly → quarterly CA3 (allowed when annual VAT < €4,000); July + December instalments → simplified regime, out of scope, stated
  • Debits with no VAT info → deductible announced as a floor, count of unknowns disclosed, no rate ever guessed
  • Receipts with no matching invoice → listed separately with amounts, never silently taxed or ignored
  • Reverse charge (EU purchases / imports): foreign counterparties detected → transactions flagged "verify with your accountant", never computed silently
  • Credit notes deducted from collected VAT · card settlement delays (emitted_at vs settled_at) · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere · non-French company or empty account: the skill says what it can and can't prepare

🏗 Functional diagram

🔒

Entirely read-only: no write tool, no transmission. The skill prepares, cross-checks and justifies — it cannot file, pay or modify anything. The only action that follows is the user's: copying the boxes into the tax portal, ideally after the accountant's review.

🛡 Guardrails

  • A preparation aid, NOT a filing: no write tool, no transmission — the skill cannot submit anything
  • Accountant validation recommended in every report — especially reverse charge, fixed assets and prior credit
  • Never invents: no French boxes for non-French organizations, no guessed VAT rate, no reverse charge computed silently
  • Deductible = announced floor · unmatched receipts listed · masked IBANs · pagination ≤ 50
