qonto-carbon-ledger

Your bank statement is secretly a carbon ledger — an approximate footprint for your business, from its real spending.

🔒 100% read-only📉 ±50% printed everywhere 📚 Sourced & dated factors📄 PR-ready

🎯 Why this matters

Small businesses never do a carbon assessment — not because they don't care, but because they have no data, no time, and no budget for a consultant. Here's the thing: bank debits ARE data. Every euro spent has a footprint, and the skill reads it straight from the Qonto account:

1

Approximate annual footprint

Debits classified into emission categories × monetary factors (kgCO2e/€, ADEME-style spend-based method) — ±50% uncertainty on every figure.

2

Dominant categories

The 2-3 categories concentrating most emissions, matched to the amounts actually spent.

3

Trend

Year over year across 24–36 months of history — is the footprint going up or down, and why.

4

Top-3 levers

The most effective actions given real spending — lever #1 is often free. Never a carbon offset for sale.

It's an orientation pre-assessment, not a regulatory report — and the skill says so itself, loudly. The first carbon number most small businesses will ever see — for two minutes of their Monday morning.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • Honest scope, announced: the spend-based method covers goods, services and energy. Excluded and said to be excluded: internal transfers, salaries, taxes, VAT payments, loan repayments, dividends — financial flows carry no emission factor
  • Normalized counterparties (case, accents, multiple spellings merged) + labels + supplier invoices — and since list_cash_flow_categories returns 403 on the claude.ai connector, the fallback is labels
  • Visible "unclassified" bucket with its share; above ~20% of spend the skill says the estimate is weak and suggests labelling the top counterparties
  • Excl-VAT amounts: vat_amount when present, else ÷ 1.20 — with the count of estimated lines disclosed
  • One-off purchases flagged apart (≥ ~€5,000): a vehicle bought once doesn't masquerade as a recurring emission
  • Short history → annualized with a warning · empty account → the skill explains the method and stops there · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere

🛡 Guardrails

  • Order-of-magnitude estimate, not a regulatory carbon report (BEGES / CSRD / GHG Protocol) — repeated in every report; for an official assessment: a specialized provider
  • Every factor shown with its source and vintage; every figure with its range — no false precision ("~9 tCO2e (4.5–13.5)", never "9.37")
  • Never sells or recommends carbon offsets
  • 100% read-only · IBANs masked (last 4 digits) · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere
  • Country ≠ FR: identical method, factors flagged as needing adaptation — no fake local calibration
