# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-carbon-ledger

Invoke with `/qonto-carbon-ledger <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started

- "What's my company's carbon footprint, estimated from my spending?"
- "Which spending categories emit the most for me?"

## Going further

- "Did my footprint go down since last year? Show the multi-year trend."
- "How much does my digital and cloud stack weigh, and which vendors drive it?"
- "What are my 3 most effective levers, given what I actually spend?"
- "How reliable is that number? Explain the method and the uncertainty."
- "Break down my travel emissions for last year only."
- "Prepare a pre-assessment I can hand to a carbon consultant — categories, amounts, factors."

## Chain it

- "Build the HTML dashboard — gauge, category bars, trend, levers."
- "The biggest lever is recurring SaaS — audit those subscriptions with qonto-subscription-audit."
- "Add a one-slide footprint summary to the next board update with qonto-board-pack."
