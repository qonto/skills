# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-brand-shield

Invoke with `/qonto-brand-shield <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started

- "Is my brand actually protected?"
- "How much have I invested in building my brand?"

## Going further

- "Total my brand spend over the last 18 months — design, domains, ads, packaging, print."
- "Suggest the Nice classes that match my real activity."
- "I checked the trademark registry — here's what I found. Fold it into the report."
- "What's my financial exposure if someone registers my brand name before me?"
- "Is my trademark's 10-year renewal coming up?"
- "Prepare my INPI pre-filing dossier — name, classes, indicative cost."

## Chain it

- "Give me the exposure one-pager as an HTML page for my co-founder."
- "Some of that brand spend is recurring — check the domains and tools with qonto-subscription-audit."
- "Dig into the design agencies behind these debits with qonto-supplier-detective."
