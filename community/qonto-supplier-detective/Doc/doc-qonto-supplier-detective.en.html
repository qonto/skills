qonto-supplier-detective

The detective for your supplier payments — duplicates, double payments, amount gaps and IBAN changes, evidence included.

🔍 100% read-only, zero writes🚨 IBAN watch vs wire fraud 🧾 Evidence on every finding📄 PR-ready

🎯 Why this matters

In most companies, a small share of supplier payments are simply mistakes — duplicates, invoices paid twice, overpayments — and nobody ever goes looking. The skill sweeps every supplier invoice and every debit, hunting 4 anomalies:

1

Duplicate invoices

Same supplier + same amount within days, or same invoice number; native has_duplicates double-checked. Subscriptions excluded by cadence detection.

2

Paid twice

1 invoice, 2 debits: the finding that pays. Partial payments and deposits recognized as legitimate — a true double payment totals ≈ 2× the invoice.

3

Invoice ↔ payment gaps

Paid more than invoiced beyond rounding; credit notes and refunds checked before flagging, foreign currency reported "to verify".

4

IBAN watch 🚨

A known supplier whose IBAN changed = a security alert pinned on top — the number-one wire-fraud pattern. Instruction: verify by phone at the known number.

Would someone run this on a Monday morning? It's the audit nobody has time to do by hand — and it can pay for itself on the first run.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • Supplier names normalized before any matching: SEPA noise, payment references, legal suffixes (SARL, SAS, GmbH…) — "OVH SAS", "OVH.COM" and "VIR SEPA OVH" merge; raw labels kept for display
  • THE false-positive trap: subscriptions — same amount every month at a regular cadence (3+ hits at ~30/90-day intervals) = legitimate recurrence, excluded before anything is flagged. True duplicates sit off-cycle: the same amount twice within days
  • Partial payments and deposits cleared first: several debits that sum to the invoice = a legitimate instalment plan, not a double payment
  • Credit notes and refunds identified up front: exculpatory evidence — a refund already received closes the case ("recovered", not "recoverable")
  • Card payments matched by emitted_at (1–2 day drift on settled_at) · status filters not exposed on this MCP → client-side filtering on status
  • No supplier invoices, no exposed IBAN, empty account: the skill says what it can and cannot check — never an invented finding

🏗 Functional diagram

🔒

100% read-only — the detective touches nothing; it hands you the case file. No Qonto write tool is ever called: nothing to approve, nothing executed. And for an IBAN change the instruction never varies: verify by phone at the number you already know — never the one in the email announcing the change.

🛡 Guardrails

  • 100% read-only: no Qonto write tool is ever called — nothing to approve, nothing executed
  • Wording discipline: "presumed", "to verify" — never "fraud" or "error" asserted as fact; every finding shows its evidence
  • The IBAN alert always carries the instruction: verify by phone at the known number, never through the contact details in the email announcing the change
  • Subscriptions, instalments, deposits, credit notes and refunds are cleared before anything is flagged
  • IBANs masked (last 4 digits) · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere · progress summarized on large sweeps
