qonto-pay-me-now

From quote to cash, no friction — an accepted quote becomes an invoice, a payment link, and money in the account.

✅ Payment links verified via MCP💳 Card & transfer payments 🔗 Trackable short link + QR📄 PR-ready

🎯 Why this matters

The freelance black hole: the client says yes to your quote… and then nothing happens. The invoice waits, the payment waits even longer — every day between the handshake and the money is cash you've earned but can't touch. The skill runs the whole cycle in one conversation:

1

Accepted quote detected

Quotes cross-checked against existing invoices — only the accepted, never-invoiced ones surface, with client, amount and date.

2

Invoice from the quote

Draft first, REAL document: reviewed line by line, sent only after your explicit confirmation.

3

Payment link + QR

The client pays by card or bank transfer in one click. Trackable Short.io short link + a QR code for printed invoices.

4

Follow-up & gentle nudge

Clicked but unpaid after 48 h → a soft reminder is proposed, drafted — never sent on its own.

Would someone use this on a Monday morning? It's what you run the second a client says yes.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • The differentiator: the official MCP docs list payment links as unsupported — they work (verified against the live API on a production account). The skill calls create_payment_link directly instead of trusting the doc
  • No accepted quotes? The skill says so and offers to start from any existing unpaid invoice — the rest of the cycle is identical
  • Payment links not activated? One-time activation explained (Qonto app), the cycle continues invoice-only
  • Short.io or Gmail absent → raw Qonto link + copy-ready text, stated plainly — no tracking silently faked
  • Client paid by plain transfer outside the link → matched in list_transactions (amount ± 0.01, counterparty, reference) and mark_client_invoice_as_paid proposed with evidence
  • Cash-basis VAT (vat_payment_condition: "on_receipts"): a faster payment literally accelerates the VAT cycle too — the skill points it out
  • Pagination ≤ 50 everywhere · invoice numbering left to the organization's settings · amounts always from the quote, never invented

🏗 Functional diagram

🔒

This skill cannot move money out: a payment link is a way to receive a payment from your client — it has nothing to do with outbound transfers (which the MCP cannot execute anyway: any transfer request requires your own SCA in the Qonto app). The only three writes: an invoice (draft first, sent after your explicit go), a payment link you can deactivate anytime in the app, and a paid-marking — always proposed, never automatic.

🛡 Guardrails

  • Invoices via MCP are REAL: draft first, sent only after explicit confirmation; rehearsals on a fictional client + delete_client_invoice afterwards
  • No outbound payments: the payment link receives, it never debits — unrelated to outbound transfers (always behind the user's own SCA in the app, outside the MCP)
  • Nothing is sent (invoice, email, nudge) without confirmation in the current conversation; never presented as sent/paid while pending
  • Amounts from the quote or invoice, never invented · masked IBANs · pagination ≤ 50
  • Honest degradation: no quotes, no payment-link feature, no Short.io, non-French account → the skill says what works and keeps going with that
