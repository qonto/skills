# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-pay-me-now

Invoke with `/qonto-pay-me-now <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started
- "Any accepted quotes I haven't invoiced yet?"
- "My quote for client Acme just got accepted — invoice it and get me paid"

## Going further
- "Create a payment link for invoice INV-2026-042"
- "Turn that accepted quote into an invoice, then give me a link the client can pay in one click"
- "Make me a short, trackable link — and a QR code for the printed version"
- "Who clicked my payment links but still hasn't paid?"
- "Did the client of invoice INV-2026-042 pay by plain bank transfer? If so, propose marking it paid"
- "It's been 48 hours since the click — draft a gentle nudge"

## Chain it
- "Escalate the ones that ignore the nudge" — hand the graduated reminders over to `qonto-invoice-chaser`
- "Start from this morning's sales call instead" — build the quote first with `qonto-meeting-invoice`
- "Show me what these incoming payments do to my cash projection" — continue with `qonto-tax-pilot`

> ⚠️ Invoices created here are real legal documents: everything stays a draft until you explicitly confirm sending — and a payment link can only receive money, never move it out.
