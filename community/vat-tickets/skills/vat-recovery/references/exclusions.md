# Exclusions from the deduction right — detail and exceptions

Common legal basis: the **admission coefficient** (CGI, ann. II, art. 205 and
206). Deduction coefficient = liability × taxation × admission. The exclusions
below make the admission coefficient **nil** (art. 206, IV-2) or partial
(art. 298, 4 of the CGI). Doctrine: the BOI-TVA-DED-30-30 series.

## 1. Passenger transport (art. 206, IV-2-5°) — BOI-TVA-DED-30-30-30

**Rule**: VAT on passenger transport cannot give rise to deduction, "whatever
the route and means used (road, rail, air, water)", extended to **ancillary
operations** (seat rental, etc.) (§ 10). Covers: taxi, private hire, train,
plane, boat, urban transport.

**Exceptions (§ 30-40)**:
- transport carried out **on behalf of a public passenger-transport company** (a
  carrier deducts the VAT charged to it by another carrier);
- **permanent transport contract** entered into by the company to bring its staff
  to the workplace;
- vessels assigned exclusively to a passenger-transport activity.

**Counter-exception (§ 50)**: a contract, even a permanent one, to transport the
**clientele** does not benefit from the derogation.

**Recharge nuance (§ 20)**: when transport costs are passed on to the client as
an element of the price of a principal service, the client deducts under
ordinary-law conditions — the costs follow the regime of the principal service,
not that of transport.

## 2. Housing of directors and staff (art. 206, IV-2-2°) — BOI-TVA-DED-30-30-10

**Rule (§ 1)**: no deduction on expenses for goods or services incurred "to
provide free housing for directors or staff" — acquisition, rental of buildings,
and, by settled doctrine, the **hotel nights** of directors/employees while
traveling.

**For the benefit of third parties → deductible**: if the housing expense
benefits third parties (clients, contributors), deduction is allowed. Mixed
third-party/employee expense: coefficient **pro rata to the number of third
parties** over all beneficiaries, with the identity and capacity of the
beneficiaries stated on the invoice (§ 1, note).

**Security exception (§ 10-40, strict interpretation)**: free housing of
**security, guarding or surveillance** staff on sites or premises (caretaker
lodges…). Refused for mere company housing, even if useful to the smooth running
of the business (§ 40).

**Do not confuse**: **restaurant** meals are not covered — business meals and
meals while traveling remain deductible under ordinary-law conditions.

## 3. Vehicles designed to transport people (art. 206, IV-2-6° and 7°) — BOI-TVA-DED-30-30-20

**Rule (§ 1)**: exclusion of "vehicles or machines, of whatever nature, designed
to transport people or for mixed use" that constitute a fixed asset (or are not
intended for resale in new condition), **and their component parts, spare parts
and accessories** (7°).

**The criterion is the DESIGN, not the use**: "one must refer not to the
conditions of use of the vehicle but to seek, given its characteristics at
acquisition, the use for which it is normally intended" (CE, doctrine restated in
the general-rule §).
- Category M (passenger cars, including estates, minivans, camper vans):
  excluded.
- Category **N** (utility vehicles: limited number of seats + preponderant
  loading capacity): **deductible** (§ 35).
- **DERIV VP** (a converted passenger car: a single row of seats + a loading
  space): **deductible** (§ 35).
- Pick-ups: depends on the characteristics (see the BOI for the detailed grid).

**Exceptions (vehicles deductible despite their design)**:
- intended for resale in new condition (dealers);
- **given on rental**, if the rental is subject to VAT and the vehicle is
  assigned exclusively to the rental activity (§ 60 of 30-30-30) — including
  courtesy vehicles giving rise to taxed remuneration (§ 70);
- M2/M3 (> 8 passenger seats) used to bring staff to work (§ 110);
- public passenger transport — including taxis and private-hire vehicles **in
  operation**, ambulances, hearses (§ 120-180);
- **driving schools**: vehicles assigned exclusively to driving instruction
  (CGI, art. 273 septies A; § 260-280);
- ski lifts / ski areas (assigned off-road vehicles); horse transport
  (fitted-out).

## 4. Services relating to excluded goods (art. 206, IV-2-10°) — BOI-TVA-DED-30-30-70

Services "of any kind, **notably rental**", relating to goods whose admission
coefficient is nil, do not give rise to deduction: rental, **repairs**,
transport, commissions and brokerage on those goods (§ 10). Explicit BOI example
(§ 20): the rental or **leasing of a passenger car** by an industrial company
gives no deduction right — except for a public-transport company using it for its
transport.

## 5. Fuels and petroleum products (art. 298, 4 of the CGI) — BOI-TVA-DED-30-30-40

A PARTIAL regime, depending on the vehicle in which the fuel is used:

| Product | Excluded vehicle (passenger car) | Non-excluded vehicle (utility) |
|---|---|---|
| Petrol | 80% deductible | 100% (since 1/1/2022) |
| Diesel, superethanol E85 | 80% deductible | 100% |
| LPG and gaseous hydrocarbons | 50% in liquid state in an excluded vehicle; 100% gaseous | 100% |
| Jet fuel | 0% for excluded aircraft | — |
| Lubricants | 0% for excluded vehicles | 100% |

- The BOI table (§ 110-130) confirms the petrol/diesel convergence at 80%/100%
  since 2022.
- **Private use = 0%**: fuel acquired for the personal travel of directors or
  employees, non-deductible (§ 70); conversely, fuel for **business travel**,
  even by means of a passenger car, is deductible (within the 80% limit) (§ 70).
- Use unknown at purchase: full deduction possible, but a self-supply is
  mandatory in case of later private use (§ 80).
- Combustibles (heating, stationary engines): deductible whatever their nature
  (§ 60).

## 6. Goods transferred without remuneration — gifts (art. 206, IV-2-3°) — BOI-TVA-DED-30-30-50

**Rule (§ 20)**: no deduction on goods transferred without remuneration or for
remuneration well below the normal price (gifts to the clientele, bonuses,
gratuities), whatever the capacity of the beneficiary.

**"Very low value" exception (§ 90)**: deduction allowed if the **unit value
incl. VAT** does not exceed the amount set by art. 28-00 A of annex IV to the CGI,
**per item, per year and per beneficiary** — €73 incl. VAT since 1/1/2021, an
amount revalued every 5 years (revaluation expected in 2026: check the value in
force before any numeric verdict). Distribution costs are incorporated into the
value.

Other derogations in the BOI: samples, advertising display stands, material
handed over for marketing needs (specific conditions).

## 7. Prohibited advertising (art. 206, IV-2-4°) — BOI-TVA-DED-30-30-60

No deduction for goods/services used for advertising prohibited by the public
health code (alcohol outside the legal framework of art. L. 3323-2 to L. 3323-5
CSP).

## 8. General limitation (art. 206, IV-2-1°)

Admission coefficient nil when the good or service is used **more than 90% for
purposes unrelated to the business**. A purely personal expense paid by the
company never gives a deduction right (and falls under other issues:
benefit-in-kind, abnormal act of management — out of the skill's scope).

## Outside the exclusions but with no VAT to recover (⚫)

Expenses that are **exempt** at the supplier — no VAT appears or can appear on
the invoice, so nothing to recover, whatever the coefficient:
- insurance (CGI, art. 261 C, 2°);
- exempt banking and financial services (art. 261 C, 1°) — unless the provider
  opts in;
- continuing professional training by a certified body (art. 261-4-4°);
- medical fees (art. 261-4-1°).
VAT that would wrongly appear on such an invoice is NOT deductible at the client
(art. 271, II-1-a: the tax must have "been able to legally appear" on the
invoice) — even if the supplier owes it by the mere fact of invoicing
(art. 283-3).


## Vehicles: what counts is the CATEGORY, not the number of seats (2026)

| Vehicle | Category | Deduction |
|---|---|---|
| Classic passenger car (sedan, SUV, estate) | M | ❌ 0% (206 IV-2-6°) |
| Van (Kangoo, Berlingo…), single-cab pick-up | N1 | ✅ 100% |
| DERIV VP (2 seats homologated N) | N | ✅ 100% |
| **Double-cab pick-up (≥ 2 rows)** | N1 but excluded | ❌ (BOI-TVA-DED-30-30-20 §36) |
| **Passenger car whose bench seat was removed after purchase** | stays M | ❌ (the conversion does not change the category) |
| Bicycle, e-bike, cargo bike | outside the scope of 6° | ✅ 100% (not a vehicle "designed to transport people") |

Exceptions to 6° (deduction restored): resale in new condition (dealers),
rental, > 8 seats for staff, **driving schools** (exclusive assignment, art. 273
septies A), ski lifts, **public passenger transport — taxis and private-hire
included** (exclusive assignment; ancillary tolerance < €7,630 incl. VAT/year and
< 30% of turnover).
