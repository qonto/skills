# VAT errors by activity profile — prioritizing the tool's checks

Source: legal analyses produced by Casus (wearecasus.co), 2026. For each detected
profile (via NAF code, labels or FEC structure): the FREQUENT errors (systemic,
to scan first) and the COSTLY ones (those that drive the big reassessments).

| Profile | Frequent (scan first) | Costly (alert loudly) |
|---|---|---|

## FRAGILE tolerances (never base a verdict on them)

- "Good faith" on a rate error: reversible into deliberate default (40%) for a
  professional in the sector.
- Late DES not sanctioned: depends on the SIE, no legal basis.
- Invoice in the director's name > €150 net "with a handwritten mention":
  fragile under audit.
- Exit cleaning = "regular" cleaning (para-hotel): OVERTURNED by CE 12-11-2025
  n° 498267.
- Key box alone = "reception of the clientele": admitted only if a physical
  welcome alternative exists.
- Quick-fixes presumption: rebuttable even with 2 documents (CJEU *FLO VENEER*,
  13-11-2025).

The SOLID tolerances (enforceable, L. 80 A LPF) are already in the other
references: § 100 voluntary disclosure, till receipt < €150 net, gifts €73,
variation ≤ 1/10th, ancillary < 5% turnover / < 10% resources, aggregation
< €250 in 2008/9.
