# Foreign VAT incurred — 2008/9 & 13th-directive refunds, exclusions by country

Source: legal analyses produced by Casus (wearecasus.co), 2026 — BOFiP and CJEU
cited. This file lets you decide "useful claim" vs "VAT definitively lost" for
any expense carrying non-French VAT.

## Directive 2008/9 (EU) — mechanics

- Filed on **impots.gouv.fr** (portal of the state of establishment).
- **Absolute time-bar: 30 September N+1** (no weekend carry-over — CJEU C-294/11
  *Elsacom*; CAA Versailles 13VE03389 *Costa Crociere*).
- Thresholds: **€400** (quarterly claim) / **€50** (annual or balance).
- Mandatory scans: invoices ≥ **€1,000** net, fuel ≥ **€250** net. Aggregation
  allowed for invoices < €250 from the same supplier.
- Processing: 4 months (up to 8 if information is requested); silence = rejection.
- Pro rata: that of the state of ESTABLISHMENT applies, within the limits of the
  exclusions of the refunding state (CJEU C-136/99 *Monte dei Paschi*).
- ⚠️ **VAT charged IN ERROR is NEVER refundable through this route**
  (BOI-TVA-DED-50-20-30-20 §20): only the supplier's corrective invoice opens a
  recovery (see formal-conditions.md).

## 13th directive (outside EU) — reciprocity

Filed before **30 June N+1**, tax representative mandatory. France requires
effective reciprocity:

| ✅ Reciprocity (claim possible) | ❌ No reciprocity (VAT lost) |
|---|---|
| Switzerland, Norway, Iceland, Israel, Serbia, Montenegro, Bosnia, North Macedonia | **United Kingdom (2026)**, United States (CE n° 259940), Canada, Japan, China, Australia, **Morocco**, UAE/GCC |

## Decision tree for a "foreign VAT" ticket

1. VAT charged in error (an operation that should have been net/reverse-charged)?
   → supplier corrective invoice, never 2008/9.
2. EU country? → table above: excluded category = lost; otherwise aggregate by
   country and compare to the €400/50 thresholds; deadline 30/09 N+1.
3. Non-EU country? → reciprocity: yes = 13th directive (30/06 N+1, representative);
   no (UK, US, Morocco, CH ok but…) = lost, say so honestly.
4. In every case: never on line 21 of the French CA3 return.
