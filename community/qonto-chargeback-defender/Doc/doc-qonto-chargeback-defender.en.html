qonto-chargeback-defender

Payment disputes end-to-end: detection, debit found, sourced defense file, deadline — you submit.

✅ Works Qonto-only🧾 Sourced evidence, never fabricated ⏱ Real dispute deadlines🌍 Universal — every Qonto country

🎯 Why this matters

A chargeback is a triple hit: the money pulled back out of the account, a fee on top (often €15–20, indicative), and a short window to respond (7–21 days) — and most merchants never respond at all. The skill turns a Qonto account into a defense desk:

1

Disputes detected

Via the Stripe/PayPal MCP when connected (reasons, real deadlines); otherwise clawback debits spotted in Qonto: dispute / chargeback / reversal + fee.

2

Debit found

The clawback matched to the original incoming payment: full money trail, charge → payout → clawback → fee.

3

Sourced file

Shopify order, proof of delivery, Gmail threads — every piece with its source, missing ones with where to find them.

4

Accounting traceability

A dated synthesis memo attached to the clawback transaction in Qonto (upload confirmed) — the debit carries its full story.

Would someone use this on a Monday morning? A chargeback notification is the Monday morning email you dread — this makes it a ten-minute task.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • Two modes, stated plainly: Stripe/PayPal MCP present → authoritative disputes, reasons and deadlines; otherwise Qonto-only — clawback debits (dispute / chargeback / reversal and variants), usually paired with a same-day fee debit from the same PSP
  • The Stripe quirk, owned: disputes are often netted inside the next payout — no separate debit line. The skill flags unusually low payouts as candidates and recommends the Stripe MCP instead of guessing
  • Card matching on emitted_at, not settled_at (1–2 days of drift)
  • Missing piece → listed as missing, with where to look — a checklist, not a shrug
  • No disputes found → the skill says so and never invents one; unknown deadlines → typical windows labeled indicative + pointer to the portal
  • Pagination ≤ 50 everywhere · list_transaction_attachments checked before any upload is reported as done

🏗 Functional diagram

🔒

The integrity model: the attached memo is a dated synthesis citing the source pieces (system · identifier · date · what each piece proves) — never a fabricated document. And submission stays human: the skill prepares everything, the user submits on the Stripe/PayPal portal. Never presented as filed.

🛡 Guardrails

  • Never fabricates evidence: the memo is a dated synthesis citing the originals — the distinction is written into the memo
  • Never says "response submitted" or "dispute won": submission happens on the Stripe/PayPal portal, by the user
  • Deadlines come from the dispute itself; generic windows are always labeled indicative
  • Upload only with explicit consent, reported after list_transaction_attachments confirms it
  • IBANs masked (last 4 digits) · pagination ≤ 50 · every example amount is invented
