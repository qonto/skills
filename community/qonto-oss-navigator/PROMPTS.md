# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-oss-navigator

Invoke with `/qonto-oss-navigator <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started

- "Where do I stand on the €10,000 EU distance-selling threshold?"
- "What VAT rate should I apply to my German customers?"

## Going further

- "Draft my OSS return for last quarter, country by country."
- "At my current pace, when will I cross the EU threshold?"
- "Show me my intra-EU B2B sales — the flows that stay out of OSS."
- "Compare the VAT I'd owe in Spain vs Italy on this quarter's sales."
- "Run the analysis without Shopify — infer customer countries from my bank data and tell me how confident you are."
- "Provision the VAT for this quarter into my tax sub-account."

## Chain it

- "Now handle my domestic French VAT for the same period with qonto-vat-return."
- "Reconcile these payouts with my store data via qonto-shopify-bridge first, then redo the country breakdown."
- "Add the OSS deadline to my radar of upcoming tax dates — qonto-tax-radar."

> ⚠️ The provision is only ever a pending transfer request created after your explicit consent, approved by you with SCA in the Qonto app — and filing the return on your national portal stays yours.
