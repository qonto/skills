# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-monthly-close

Invoke with `/qonto-monthly-close <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started
- "Close my month"
- "Run the month-end review for June"

## Going further
- "Anything unusual on my account last month? Duplicates, new beneficiaries, odd spend?"
- "Which receipts am I missing, biggest stakes first?"
- "What's my VAT estimate for the month — collected versus deductible?"
- "How did June compare to May, category by category?"
- "Which client invoices are still unpaid, and how late is each one?"
- "Give me the fix-it list, sorted by what costs me money first"

## Chain it
- "Hunt down the missing receipts from the to-do list" — hand over to `qonto-receipt-hunter`
- "Chase the unpaid invoices you flagged" — continue with `qonto-invoice-chaser`
- "Provision the month's taxes now that the numbers are in" — finish with `qonto-tax-pilot`
