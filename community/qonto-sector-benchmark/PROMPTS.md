# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-sector-benchmark

Invoke with `/qonto-sector-benchmark <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started
- "Am I normal? Benchmark me against my sector"
- "How does my company compare to my industry?"

## Going further
- "Do my clients pay me slower than the sector average?"
- "Am I paying too much for software for a company my size?"
- "How heavy are my fixed costs compared to businesses like mine?"
- "How many days of expenses does my cash cover — and is that comfortable for my sector?"
- "Compare me to myself a year ago — same ratios, trend arrows"
- "Redo the comparison at the broader industry level — I'm not sure my activity code is right"

## Chain it
- "My clients do pay slowly — set up the chase" — hand over to `qonto-invoice-chaser`
- "Audit that oversized software spend line" — dig in with `qonto-subscription-audit`
- "Put these ratios next to my live numbers" — open `qonto-ceo-cockpit`
