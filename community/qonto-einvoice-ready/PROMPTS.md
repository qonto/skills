# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-einvoice-ready

Invoke with `/qonto-einvoice-ready <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started

- "Am I ready for the September 2026 e-invoicing mandate?"
- "Audit my client records for Factur-X — what's missing?"

## Going further

- "Which e-invoicing timeline applies to my company — 2026 or 2027 for issuing?"
- "Which clients would block a Factur-X invoice today, field by field?"
- "Am I exposed to e-reporting? Check my foreign and B2C flows."
- "Find this client's SIREN in the public registry and show me before writing anything."
- "Complete my client records — SIREN, intra-EU VAT, structured address — with a line-by-line preview."
- "Re-score my readiness after the fixes and give me the action plan for what's left."

## Chain it

- "Give me the before/after readiness report as an HTML page for my accountant."
- "Package the clean client file into this quarter's handoff with qonto-accountant-handoff."
- "While we're at it, check my own VAT obligations for the period with qonto-vat-return."

> ⚠️ Client records are only updated after a line-by-line preview and your explicit confirmation — homonyms are asked about, never guessed.
