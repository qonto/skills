qonto-asset-registry

Your account knows what you own — the asset register nobody keeps.

🔒 100% read-only — zero writes🧠 Recognized from the first occurrence 📎 Receipts linked🛡 Insurance inventory ready

🎯 Why this matters

Nobody keeps the fixed-asset register. And the day something gets stolen or the office floods, the insurer asks for the inventory with purchase values and receipts — which nobody has. Yet the Qonto account already knows everything the company owns. The skill reads it:

1

Equipment detection

24–36 months of real purchases (transactions + supplier invoices): IT, furniture, machines, vehicles, tools, telephony — recognized from the first occurrence.

2

€500 pre-tax threshold

French tax practice: below = expense, above = fixed asset (configurable). Leasing flagged "financed", never counted.

3

The asset register

Date, supplier, description, pre-tax/incl-tax amount, linked receipt, status — with a receipt-completeness gauge.

4

Two golden outputs

Indicative depreciation (straight-line, estimated net book value — for the accountant to validate) and the insurance inventory, ready to send.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • Cryptic transaction labels → supplier invoices (list_supplier_invoices), matched by amount and date, carry the clean description and the pre-tax amount
  • Generalist merchant (marketplace, supermarket) → line "❓ to confirm" unless the label or the attached receipt makes the nature clear — never guessed
  • Installment purchases → equal payments to the same vendor merged into one asset at full value
  • Leasing / hire-purchase → recurring monthly payments to a financer: "🔁 financed, not capitalized here", excluded from totals
  • Missing receipt → line flagged 📎 with the count disclosed, and qonto-receipt-hunter suggested to recover them
  • Card purchases dated by emitted_at (settlement lags 1–2 days) · untagged VAT → estimated pre-tax amount, disclosed as such · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere

🔒

100% read-only — the strongest guarantee: no write tool is ever called. No category changed, no document uploaded, no request created. The register writes itself; the accountant keeps the pen — the €500 threshold and the depreciation lives are customary indications, not accounting entries.

Register statuses

StatusMeaning
✅ Fixed assetEquipment ≥ threshold (€500 pre-tax by default), registered and depreciated
💰 ExpensedBelow the threshold: expensed, listed for the record
❓ To confirmGeneralist merchant, nature undeterminable — the user decides
🔁 FinancedLeasing detected: flagged, never counted in the total
📎 MissingNo linked receipt — pointer to qonto-receipt-hunter

Disposals and write-offs are declared by the user, never assumed.

🛡 Guardrails

  • 100% read-only — no write tool is ever called
  • €500 threshold and depreciation lives = customary indications, not accounting entries — the accountant makes the final call, restated in every report
  • Ambiguous nature → "to confirm", never invented; disposals declared by the user, never assumed
  • Example figures are invented and presented as such · IBANs masked · pagination ≤ 50
