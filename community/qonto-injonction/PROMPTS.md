# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-injonction

Invoke with `/qonto-injonction <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started

- "This client will never pay — prepare the payment-order file for their invoice."
- "Take invoice INV-2026-017 to court: build the injonction de payer dossier."

## Going further

- "Compute the late-payment interest and the €40 recovery indemnity on this invoice."
- "Is my debtor in collective proceedings? Run the BODACC check first."
- "Which commercial court has jurisdiction over this client?"
- "The client paid part of it in April — deduct the partial payment and redo the claim."
- "This debtor just filed for bankruptcy — what do I do instead of the injunction?"
- "Build the numbered exhibit list from my Qonto receipts and tell me what's missing."

## Chain it

- "Before going legal, check how much time I have left on my other receivables — qonto-prescription-guard."
- "For the invoices that are late but not desperate yet, run reminders with qonto-invoice-chaser instead."
- "Give me the printable dossier as an HTML page I can save as PDF and sign."
