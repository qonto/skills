qonto-injonction

When reminders stop working — a French payment-order court file, ready to sign, built from the Qonto account.

⚖️ Pre-filled CERFA 12946🔒 Zero writes, by design 🏛 BODACC checked first📄 PR-ready

🎯 Why this matters

Reminders failed (see qonto-invoice-chaser), the formal notice went unanswered. The French payment-order procedure costs ~€35 and needs no lawyer — yet almost nobody dares: an intimidating form, interest to compute, the right court to find. Most founders just write the invoice off. The skill builds the whole file from the Qonto account:

1

BODACC check first

Public API, no key: debtor in collective proceedings → NO injunction, the skill reorients to a proof of claim (2-month deadline) and drafts it.

2

The claim, to the day

Principal (payments on account deducted), interest at ECB + 10 pts (floor 3× the French legal rate — art. L441-10), the €40 indemnity — formula shown.

3

Complete dossier

CERFA 12946 pre-filled field by field, numbered exhibit list (Qonto receipts), competent commercial court identified from the debtor's registered office.

4

Infogreffe filing guide

Online filing ~€35, exhibits as PDF < 2 MB, and what happens next: order → service → 1-month opposition. Filing is the user's move.

Would someone use this on a Monday morning? It's the Monday you finally stop waiting for that invoice.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • The invoice might already be paid: credit-side scan since the issue date — an unreconciled transfer is caught, the skill stops and proposes reconciliation instead of a lawsuit
  • Partial payments: detected, listed as exhibits and deducted from the principal — only the balance is claimed
  • No SIREN on the client record: registry lookup by name + city, match confirmed by the user (homonyms) — never assumed
  • Debtor in liquidation: automatic switch to the proof of claim, with its 2-month deadline stated
  • Receipt missing from Qonto: listed as "📋 to provide" in the exhibit list — never invented
  • Disputed claim or limitation risk (5 years, art. L110-4): flagged, never silenced — see qonto-prescription-guard
  • No web access, foreign debtor, empty account: the skill says exactly what it can and can't build · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere

🏗 Functional diagram

🔒

Zero writes, by design: the skill reads Qonto and two keyless public APIs, computes, assembles — and stops at a ready-to-sign dossier. Nothing is filed, nothing is served, nothing is signed. Filing on Infogreffe is the user, with their own signature. No legal act is ever executed by an AI.

🛡 Guardrails

  • Not legal advice — stated on every dossier; interest = indicative computation to be validated (lawyer, court bailiff, or the court clerk)
  • Zero writes: never presented as filed, served, or granted — the skill stops at the ready-to-sign dossier
  • Debtor in collective proceedings → the injunction is never suggested; systematic reorientation to the proof of claim
  • Disputed claims and limitation risks are flagged, never silenced
  • France-only procedure; elsewhere, a factual dossier without a form
  • IBANs masked (last 4 digits) · pagination ≤ 50 · examples invented (INV-YYYY-NNN)
