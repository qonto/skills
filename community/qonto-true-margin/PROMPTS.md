# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-true-margin

Invoke with `/qonto-true-margin <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started

- "What's my true margin per product, based on what actually leaves my bank account?"
- "Which products am I selling at a loss?"

## Going further

- "My dashboard says 40% margin on this product — is that real once PSP fees, FX and shipping are in?"
- "Allocate my costs pro-rata to units sold instead of revenue and compare."
- "How much do payment fees and currency conversion really cost me per month?"
- "Propose a price that targets a 25% contribution margin on my worst product."
- "Show me the waterfall from dashboard margin to real margin for my top seller."
- "Which cost family hurts the most — PSP, FX, shipping or supplier COGS?"

## Chain it

- "Apply the new price for that one product — I'll validate each product separately."
- "Now that I know the real margins, size the next restock with qonto-cash-to-stock."
- "Reconcile the store payouts behind these numbers with qonto-shopify-bridge."

> ⚠️ Prices are only changed in your store after line-by-line validation, one product at a time, and each change is re-read and confirmed before/after.
