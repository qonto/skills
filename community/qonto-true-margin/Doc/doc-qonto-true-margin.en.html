qonto-true-margin

Pricing from real banking costs — the dashboard's margin is a fiction; the bank account knows the real one.

🔒 Zero write on the Qonto side🛒 Shopify prices applied line by line 🧮 Allocation announced as a heuristic🌍 Universal — all Qonto countries

🎯 Why this matters

Every e-commerce dashboard shows a margin built from theoretical costs. What actually leaves the bank account — PSP fees really debited, FX costs, real carrier invoices, supplier purchases — tells a different story. The skill rebuilds the truth, then turns it into a pricing decision:

1

Real costs, classified

Observed PSP fees (or "unknown, not zero" when netted inside payouts), FX, carriers, supplier COGS — tagged 🟢 observed · 🟡 derived · ⚪ unknown.

2

True margin per product

Shopify per-product sales − real costs allocated pro-rata (revenue or units) — the method is printed on every table.

3

Loss-makers identified

Sorted first, with the quantified gap between the dashboard's theoretical margin and the real one.

4

New prices, validated

Break-even + target price; applied in Shopify (update-product) only after explicit line-by-line validation.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • PSP fees netted inside payouts (the Shopify Payments case: no separate debit) → effective rate derived from gross (store) vs net (bank) when the Shopify MCP is present, otherwise shown as "unknown, not zero" — never an announced rate passed off as observed
  • Allocation = a stated heuristic: pro-rata revenue (default) or units, method printed on every table; supplier invoices naming a product are matched directly, allocation covers only the rest
  • No Shopify MCP: announced degraded mode — true margin by cost family, no per-product claim
  • Card-paid shipping: reconciled on emitted_at (1–2 day drift vs settled_at) · refunds deducted from net revenue · foreign-currency debits → real FX cost isolated
  • Thin history (< 3 months): lower confidence tags + a warning, not fake precision
  • Pagination ≤ 50 everywhere · counterparty spellings normalized and merged

🏗 Functional diagram

🔒

On the Qonto side: read-only. No banking write tool exists in this skill — nothing can move on the account. The only write is on the Shopify side (price updates): each product requires explicit validation in the conversation, a global "apply everything" is refused, and the price is re-read after application before being reported.

🧾 Real-cost families (read from the Qonto debits)

FamilySourceQuirk handled

FXDebits whose local currency ≠ account currency (local_amount/local_currency) + explicit fee linesReal conversion cost isolated, never estimated from a public rate
ShippingCarrier counterparties (Colissimo, Chronopost, Mondial Relay, DHL, UPS, Sendcloud…)Card payments reconciled on emitted_at (1–2 day drift)
Supplier COGSlist_supplier_invoices + recurring supplier debitsInvoice → product matched directly when named, otherwise pro-rata allocation
Fixed costsEverything else (rent, SaaS, salaries)Excluded from contribution margin, disclosed separately — stated plainly

🛡 Guardrails

  • NEVER applies a price without explicit validation of that specific product in the current conversation; never reported as done without a Shopify re-read
  • The allocation is a stated heuristic — method (pro-rata revenue or units) printed on every table; true margins are estimates, not accounting
  • Suggestion ≠ pricing strategy: elasticity not modeled — said honestly with every proposal
  • Zero Qonto write tool · invisible fees → "unknown, not zero" · masked IBANs · pagination ≤ 50 · per-currency reporting
