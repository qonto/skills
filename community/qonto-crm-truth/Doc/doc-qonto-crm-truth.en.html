qonto-crm-truth

The CRM that stops lying — the bank as the source of truth, written into HubSpot or Airtable.

🏦 Bank truth vs CRM fiction🤝 Matching always confirmed ✍️ Writes to HubSpot or Airtable🔒 Zero Qonto writes

🎯 Why this matters

In every CRM, a deal marked "Closed Won" looks like revenue. Until the money hits the account, it's fiction — and sales teams prioritize on numbers the bank never confirmed. The skill reconciles CRM records (HubSpot or Airtable) with the Qonto account:

1

Won-but-never-cashed deals

Every won deal with no matching euro on the account — listed with amount, age and CRM record.

2

Real revenue, LTV & delays

Cash actually collected (not declared), cash LTV, the observed "pays at day +X" delay, the last payment date.

3

A/B/C payer score

Computed from bank data, formula disclosed — never a guessed score (< 2 paid invoices → unrated).

4

Written into the CRM

HubSpot custom properties / Airtable columns — after confirmed matching and an approved preview, never silent.

Would someone use this on a Monday morning? It's the sales pipeline review — with the bank's numbers.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • Multiple spellings merged: "ACME STUDIO", "Acme Studio SARL", "acme studio"… normalized (case, accents, legal suffixes) and shown as ONE client in the mapping table
  • CRM record with no Qonto counterpart (or vice versa) → listed as unmatched, excluded from writes, never force-matched
  • Deals without amounts → flagged as unquantified instead of guessed; partial payments and instalments cumulated per invoice; refunds deducted from revenue
  • Fewer than 2 paid invoices → unrated, with the reason — never an invented grade
  • No CRM connected → stated plainly; the scored-clients report is delivered in the conversation (already useful)
  • Empty account: nothing invented — the skill says what it cannot compute · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere

🏗 Functional diagram

🔒

Zero bank writes: on the Qonto side the skill is pure read — it cannot touch money. The only writes target the CRM, behind two locks: the CRM-record ↔ Qonto-client matching, always shown and confirmed (never silent), then the exact preview of every value, explicitly approved in the conversation.

🅰️ The payer score (disclosed formula, never a black box)

ScoreDefinition
AMedian settlement within 5 days of the due date, nothing currently overdue
BAlways pays, but late — median lateness ≤ 30 days past due
CMedian lateness > 30 days, or an invoice unpaid > 60 days, or a won deal never cashed
—Fewer than 2 paid invoices → unrated (never a guessed score)

⚠️ The score describes payment behavior observed on this account — not general creditworthiness; restated in every report.

🛡 Guardrails

  • Zero Qonto writes — pure read on the bank side; the only writes target the CRM
  • Never writes to the CRM without (a) a confirmed mapping table and (b) an explicit go on an exact preview; never presented as done if it failed or was partial
  • Assisted matching, never silent — ambiguous pairs become questions
  • Honest degradation: unrated under 2 paid invoices · counterparty-level without invoices · conversation report without a CRM
  • IBANs masked (last 4 digits) · pagination ≤ 50 · rehearse writes on a duplicated Airtable base or test properties (CRM writes are real)
