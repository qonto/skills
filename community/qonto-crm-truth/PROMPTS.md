# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-crm-truth

Invoke with `/qonto-crm-truth <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started

- "Which of my closed-won deals never actually got paid?"
- "Score my clients by how they actually pay, not by what the CRM says."

## Going further

- "Is my top CRM account really my top payer? Compare the two rankings."
- "What's Acme's average payment delay — median and mean, observed from the bank?"
- "Which clients are drifting — old last payments, growing delays?"
- "Compute real revenue and LTV per client over the last 24 months."
- "No CRM connected — just score my Qonto clients in the conversation."
- "Sync the payer scores and real revenue back into my CRM — show me the field-by-field preview first."

## Chain it

- "Chase the unpaid invoices behind those won-not-cashed deals with qonto-invoice-chaser."
- "For the C-rated payers, send payment links up front next time — qonto-pay-me-now."
- "Fold the real-revenue picture into this month's board update with qonto-board-pack."

> ⚠️ Nothing is written to your CRM before you confirm the entity matching and give an explicit go on the previewed fields — and only skill-dedicated fields are touched, never native ones.
