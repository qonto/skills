qonto-rgpd-register

Your bank account knows your data processors better than you do — the GDPR Article 30 register, drafted from your spending.

🔍 100% read — zero write🇪🇺 Pan-European (GDPR = the whole EU) 🗂 CNIL card format, card by card⚠️ Draft to validate (DPO/lawyer)

🎯 Why this matters

The record of processing activities (GDPR Article 30) is the document 90% of small businesses don't have — and the first thing a supervisory authority asks for. Yet the list of processors handling your personal data is already written somewhere: in your debits. The skill reads it:

1

Processor detection

The SaaS tools that process personal data (CRM, email marketing, analytics, AI, cloud, payroll, support) — recognized from their first occurrence, Claude's general knowledge.

2

Classification by activity

Purpose, typical data categories, data subjects, EU / non-EU headquarters and hosting — every attribute tagged "to validate".

3

Pre-filled Article 30 register

One card per activity in the CNIL format, controller header filled from get_organization, visible placeholders for the human decisions.

4

Actionable flags

🔴 non-EU transfer → SCCs to verify · 🟠 DPA to locate or sign · 🟡 tool gone from the debits for 6 months → retire it from the register?

Would someone use this on a Monday morning? It's the Monday a client's security questionnaire — or an authority letter — lands.

⚙️ How it works

Holds up on messy data

  • Recognition from the first occurrence: Claude's general knowledge identifies the vendor (HQ, role, typical data) — no recurrence threshold, so yearly-billed tools are caught too
  • Spellings normalized and merged: GOOGLE *WORKSPACE, GOOGLE IRELAND LTD, Google Cloud EMEA = one vendor; cross-checked with list_supplier_invoices (cleaner legal names than card labels)
  • Nothing silently disappears: out-of-scope spend (bank fees, travel, hardware) is listed in an "out of scope" appendix
  • Ambiguity stated, never decided: genuinely ambiguous roles (payment providers are often independent controllers) → announced as such, "to validate"
  • Vendor masked by a reseller, marketplace or app store → 🟡 flag, the skill asks instead of guessing
  • Unknown vendor → "unidentified — check the invoice", never an invented HQ · pagination ≤ 50 everywhere · emitted_at (not settled_at) for the last-used date

🏗 Functional diagram

🔒

Zero write, by design: four read tools, no write tool. Nothing moves on the account, no vendor is contacted. And the document produced is a draft register, not legal advice — qualification, legal bases and retention periods are validated by a DPO or lawyer. The skill repeats this on every output.

🛡 Guardrails

  • Never a definitive legal qualification (processor vs controller vs joint, legal basis, retention) — every card is tagged "to validate", undecided fields stay visible placeholders
  • Never invents vendor facts: unknown HQ or hosting → stated as unknown, "check the invoice / the vendor's DPA page" as the next step
  • A register draft ≠ GDPR compliance: one required document, not the whole program — DPO/legal validation recommended in every report
  • 100% read, zero write · IBANs masked (last 4 digits) · pagination ≤ 50 · all examples invented
