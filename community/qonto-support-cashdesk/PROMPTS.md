# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-support-cashdesk

Invoke with `/qonto-support-cashdesk <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started

- "A customer says they never received invoice INV-2026-042 — here's their message: [...]"
- "Handle this billing complaint: [pasted conversation]"

## Going further

- "This client says their payment link expired: [...] — check the link's real status and fix it."
- "A customer claims they were charged twice — here's the thread. Prove it or disprove it from the credits."
- "Handle the open Intercom ticket from Acme about their invoice."
- "Before doing anything, show me the evidence: invoice status, dates, incoming payments."
- "The double charge is proven — prepare the credit note and the reply explaining it."
- "Draft the customer answer, factual and calm, with the fix attached — I'll send it myself."

## Chain it

- "The invoice is real and simply unpaid — switch to reminders with qonto-invoice-chaser."
- "They want to pay right now — issue a fresh link via qonto-pay-me-now."
- "If this dispute escalates into a chargeback, prepare the defense with qonto-chargeback-defender."

> ⚠️ Every fix — resend, fresh link, credit note — is explicitly confirmed by you first, and the customer reply always stays a draft you review and send yourself.
