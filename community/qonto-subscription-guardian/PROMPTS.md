# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-subscription-guardian

Invoke with `/qonto-subscription-guardian <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started
- "Protect my subscriptions with capped virtual cards"
- "List my subscriptions — which ones aren't guarded yet?"

## Going further
- "Cap my design-software subscription — it keeps creeping up"
- "Give my three biggest subscriptions a dedicated card each, with a 10% margin"
- "That vendor bills yearly — size the cap to the annual renewal, not the monthly"
- "Which SUB- cards already exist, and what are their limits?"
- "How many virtual cards does my plan still allow before this costs extra?"
- "Lock the card for the tool we stopped using — but tell me what happens vendor-side first"

## Chain it
- "Run the full recurring-spend audit first — cost, zombies, price hikes" — that's `qonto-subscription-audit`'s job
- "Watch for the vendor's next charge and flag anything above the cap" — add `qonto-fraud-sentinel` to your mornings
- "Show me how the subscription line looks in my company dashboard" — open `qonto-ceo-cockpit`

> ⚠️ Every card is created one at a time, with your explicit confirmation for that specific card — and it only exists once you approve the request yourself with 2FA in the Qonto app.
