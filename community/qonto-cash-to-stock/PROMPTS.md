# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-cash-to-stock

Invoke with `/qonto-cash-to-stock <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started

- "How many units can I reorder without putting the account in danger, and by when?"
- "When will I run out of stock?"

## Going further

- "What's my truly free cash once the next 90 days of commitments are paid?"
- "Show me the commitments calendar — what's causing the low point in the cash curve?"
- "What if I split the restock into two smaller orders?"
- "What if I wait until after the VAT deadline to order?"
- "My supplier needs 30 days lead time and the landed cost is around €12 a unit — how many units can I fund?"
- "Run the restock analysis without Shopify — estimate my sales velocity from receipts only."

## Chain it

- "OK, prepare the supplier transfer request for the order we just sized."
- "Before I order, check this supplier's track record with qonto-supplier-detective."
- "Now show me which of those products are actually worth restocking — what's their real margin? (qonto-true-margin)"

> ⚠️ This skill never moves money on its own: the supplier transfer is only ever prepared as a pending request after your explicit go, and you approve it yourself with SCA in the Qonto app.
