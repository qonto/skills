# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-supplier-detective

Invoke with `/qonto-supplier-detective <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started
- "Run the supplier audit — find the money leaking out"
- "Did I pay anything twice this year?"

## Going further
- "Hunt for duplicate invoices in my supplier pile"
- "Has any supplier changed their IBAN recently? That's the one check I never do"
- "Compare invoice INV-2026-042 with what I actually paid — any gap?"
- "Run a mini-audit on just this one supplier"
- "How much could I realistically claim back, and with what evidence?"
- "That recurring charge is a subscription, not a duplicate — clear it and update the total"

## Chain it
- "Draft the claim email for the top finding, with both transfer references"
- "Check whether that supplier is in legal trouble too" — cross-check with `qonto-counterparty-watch`
- "Keep an eye on new suspicious debits going forward" — add `qonto-fraud-sentinel` to your mornings
