# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-receipt-hunter

Invoke with `/qonto-receipt-hunter <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started
- "Which transactions are missing receipts?"
- "Find my missing receipts for June and hunt them down"

## Going further
- "How much recoverable VAT am I at risk of losing to missing receipts?"
- "Search my inbox for the receipt behind that ride-hailing charge from last week"
- "Go through the whole quarter, biggest stakes first"
- "Look in my Drive too — some supplier invoices land there as PDFs"
- "Hunt them all down and propose the attachments one by one"
- "What's my receipt completeness score before and after this session?"

## Chain it
- "Now prepare the pack for my accountant with the receipts attached" — hand over to `qonto-accountant-handoff`
- "Re-check how the missing receipts affect my deductible VAT" — run `qonto-vat-return`
- "Close the month now that the receipts are in" — continue with `qonto-monthly-close`

> ⚠️ This skill attaches receipts to transactions only after you confirm each match — one confirmation per attachment, originals only, never in bulk.
