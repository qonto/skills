# 💬 Example prompts — qonto-chargeback-defender

Invoke with `/qonto-chargeback-defender <your request>` — or just ask in plain language: the skill's description triggers it automatically.

## Getting started

- "Any open payment disputes on my account?"
- "I just got a chargeback — walk me through it."

## Going further

- "Why did my payment processor take money back last month? Trace the clawback."
- "Find the debit that matches this dispute — original payment, payout, clawback, fee."
- "Build the defense file for this dispute: order, delivery proof, customer emails."
- "Which evidence pieces am I missing, and where do I find them?"
- "When is my response due for this chargeback, exactly?"
- "Draft the response in the structure the processor's form expects."

## Chain it

- "Attach the dated evidence memo to the clawback transaction so accounting keeps the trail."
- "The customer is also complaining by email — handle the billing side with qonto-support-cashdesk."
- "If this looks like friendly fraud, log the pattern with qonto-fraud-sentinel."

> ⚠️ The memo is only attached to the Qonto transaction after your explicit consent — and submitting the defense on the processor's portal is always done by you.
